Euclid

Pick a style and every drum channel is filled in for you. Energy thins or busies the hats and ghost notes, the dice makes another take. Then tweak steps in the Channel rack, or just press Space.

Tap the pads, or use the keys 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V. With Record on, hits snap to the nearest 16th and land in the Channel rack. Hold a pad with Note repeat for rolls.

Press Record on a track: it waits for the next bar, records the mic and/or everything the song and pads play, then loops in time. Press it again to overdub. Use headphones with the mic on.
