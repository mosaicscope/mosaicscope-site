This derived catalogue contains the GeoNames identifier, name, country code,
latitude, longitude and recorded population. Selection: population at least
100,000, plus all cities15000 entries in NZ, AU, KW, AE, BH, QA, OM and SA.
Population values and geographic coverage reflect the source dataset.
City centres identify starting districts; they do not describe building facades.
Building footprints, available heights, roads and venues are obtained separately
from OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL 1.0, with in-game attribution.
