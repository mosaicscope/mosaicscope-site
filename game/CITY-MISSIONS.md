# In-world missions

Random travel-triggered choice stories no longer generate or open a panel. Explore no longer contains the old two-choice encounter cards; pending events and scene overlays are cleared when loading old saves. Historical outcomes remain in saves. Optional city activities and the normal city simulation continue.

Missions is a visible action on desktop and mobile. Accepting a job returns to normal play with a HUD objective, a world marker and map/minimap markers. Three courier jobs use suitable existing entrances; the driving circuit uses three distinct mapped streets. Unavailable maps report why a route cannot start. Nothing starts automatically.

For delivery jobs, stand on foot at the pickup entrance for one second, travel normally, then stand at the destination entrance. Parcels appear at pickup points and beside the player while carried on foot. Handovers stop while away, indoors, in a car or incapacitated. Driving checkpoints require a car. Missions have no countdown or reaction-click sequence. Progress and completion are saved; abandoning pays nothing and completion pays once. Discontinuous relocation does not count as delivery travel.

Validation covers actual mission acceptance on the city map, keyboard and real multi-touch joystick travel in an unobstructed movement fixture, automatic pickup/delivery, legacy interruption suppression, saved progression, malformed saves, physical checkpoints and duplicate reward prevention, plus existing city/driving regressions.

Rollback source and generated world assets together. Old saves without mission data remain valid.
