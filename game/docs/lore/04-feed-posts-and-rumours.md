# Lore notes 4: the feed, the posts and the rumours

Not every piece of the story is said in a conversation. A lot of it is posted: the live feed Terrance runs, the Mosaicscope account's captions, the local papers, the Council's notices and the rumours that go round the car yards. These are the surfaces the player reads without being spoken to, and they are where the city's tone is most obvious. They should be short, a little ridiculous, and occasionally true.

This note lists the kinds of posts the game can show, the voice each one should use, and a set of drafts. Like the exchange drafts in `03-clue-exchange-drafts.md`, nothing here is wired in yet. The `{post}` placeholder can carry a real recent caption, but only in exchanges tagged `post`, as the merge script enforces.

## The voices

**Terrance.** The live feed. Terrance is calm, quick and never quite sure of the source. Posts are short, lower-case where it feels right, and end with the time rather than a sign-off. Terrance never says "according to sources". Terrance says what happened, and the city decides if it believes it.

**The Mosaicscope account.** The crew's own account. Chatty, a little boastful, and wrong about dates. Captions use the crew's catchphrases ("Run it back" for Tiles, "Get my good side" for Glitter). The account never posts about the cones. It posts about parties, and the parties are the only thing it gets right.

**The Council.** Formal, slightly defensive, and full of consultation. Council notices always mention a survey. Notices always promise a review. Nothing is ever completed on time, and the notices say so in small print.

**The local paper.** Dry and weary, written by people who have seen every council meeting. The paper's stories are the places where the lore is most explicit, but they are also the least reliable, because the paper prints what the Council tells it.

**The car yards.** Rumour, mostly. Posted as classified ads. The ads are for things nobody should be selling, and the sellers are always "a friend". The Pink Pukeko is advertised here more often than any other car.

## Terrance feed posts

These should be posted over the course of a day, roughly one an hour, so the player sees them accumulate. A post that appears after a related clue should feel like confirmation, not explanation.

- "Ice on the Avon, forecast says no. Forecast is wrong. 6:14am."
- "Cones on the Bridge of Remembrance, new pattern, spiral. Council says survey. 9:02am."
- "Kiwi-signal over the Port Hills, first time this week. Bryce not commenting. 9:41pm."
- "Two Pies closed for the afternoon. Sal says 'the pies are fine, it's the interest.' 2:20pm."
- "Power cut at the Stage at midnight. Static says it was the signal. Static is not a reliable narrator. 12:03am."
- "Kevin the giraffe has eaten a stack of paper. Zookeeper says the paper had numbers on it. 11:15am."
- "Pink Pukeko listed again at the Moorhouse yards. Price unchanged since last year. 10:30am."
- "Hubcaps reported missing from cars near the bridge. Riccarton Boys deny involvement, again. 4:48pm."
- "Council announces another review of the cones. Results expected after the next review. 3:00pm."
- "The tab is closed. Gina has written the last line. The pies are still very large. 7:30pm."

Terrance should never post the same line twice in a day, and should never post a clue without a time stamp. The time stamp is part of the joke.

## Mosaicscope account captions

These are posted by the crew's account. Each caption is a little wrong about something the player has seen.

- "Party at the Plaza tonight. Tiles says bring a ball. Tiles has no ball." (Tiles, morning)
- "Glitter knows what you did. Glitter is not telling. Yet." (Glitter, afternoon)
- "Kodak got the shot. We are not allowed to say which shot." (Kodak, evening)
- "Nova says it's going to rain. Nova is never wrong about rain." (Nova, morning)
- "Grain's prank did not go to plan. Grain is 'working on it'." (Grain, afternoon)
- "Flash has not posted in a while. He is fine. He is at the playground." (Flash, evening)
- "Ruby has made a new toy from a traffic cone. We did not ask where the cone came from." (Ruby, day)
- "Bando challenged the whole court to a rematch. The court did not respond." (Bando, afternoon)
- "Lux has learned the name of the busker's dog. The dog is delighted." (Lux, morning)
- "Static's boombox is charged. Static would like it known that the backup battery is also charged." (Static, night)

## Council notices

Each notice is short and polite and includes a promise.

- "Notice of consultation: the Bridge of Remembrance cone pattern. Please submit your views by Friday."
- "The Council thanks residents for their patience during the cone review. Patience is appreciated."
- "A new bench has been installed on the Plaza following community consultation. The bench is not for sleeping."
- "The Council regrets to inform residents that the Avon will not freeze this season. This is subject to review."
- "Ransom payments have been reviewed and found to be in order. No further review is planned until the next review."

## Local paper headlines

The paper runs a few headlines a week. The best ones are the ones that almost say what is happening.

- "Council Defends Cones: 'They Are Doing Their Job'"
- "Scientist Says Avon Freeze 'Not a Weapon, Just a Jar'"
- "Giraffe Eats Paper; Zookeeper Blames Survey"
- "Famiglia Interest Rates Remain 'Competitive', Says Insider"
- "Hubcap Thefts Continue; Riccarton Boys 'Unbothered'"
- "Signal Over Port Hills: Bryce 'Only Helping a Cat'"

## Car yard ads

Classified ads. The sellers are always "a friend".

- "PINK PUKEKO. Good condition. Only slightly pink. Serious buyers only, a friend says."
- "HUBCAPS, assorted, good quality. Unlikely to be yours. Cash, no questions. A friend."
- "Ute with tow bar, reliable. Previously owned by the Famiglia. Previously is doing a lot of work here."
- "Garden gnomes, sixty, nearly new. Nobody asks where they came from. A friend has them."
- "Lost: one cat. Very lost. Reward offered by a superhero, who is definitely not involved."

## Rumours

Rumours are passed between people, and they change as they go. The game can show a rumour in one form in one place and a different form elsewhere, so the player learns that the city disagrees with itself.

- The cones were a Council prank that got out of hand.
- The cones are a survey, and the answers are being sold to the car yards.
- The freeze is real, and Quakenstein has a second jar.
- The freeze is fake, and the Council is paying for the jar.
- The signal is a cat.
- The signal is Bryce, who is not a cat person.
- The Don is planning to buy the Pink Pukeko.
- The Don has already bought the Pink Pukeko, and nobody has seen it.
- Kevin knows where the numbers went.
- Kevin ate the numbers, and that is the end of it.

## How the feed and the rumours fit the clues

- The feed carries the clues that are facts: times, places and what was seen. A player who reads the feed should be able to reconstruct the timeline in Act 2 without talking to anyone.
- The account and the car yards carry the clues that are opinions: who the crew trusts, what people want, and what they will pay for.
- The Council and the paper carry the clues that are lies, or at least half-truths. A player who believes them should be led somewhere wrong, and then have a reason to doubt them.
- The rumours carry the disagreements. They are never resolved by the game. The player chooses which version to believe.

## Rules for the writers

1. Every post has a time, unless it is a rumour. A rumour should never have a time stamp.
2. No post explains the story. Posts can point at it, but the player has to do the joining.
3. Keep the posts short enough to read on a phone. The longest in these notes is about 90 characters.
4. Let the Council be wrong in a way that is funny. It should never be wrong in a way that is cruel.
5. Let the feed be right, but never let it be the only source. Terrance is a witness, not a judge.
6. Use the car yard ads for jokes and for hints. They are the quickest way to show that the city is trading in things it should not be.

## Open questions

- Should the feed be readable only in the game, or also on the Mosaicscope site as a real page?
- Should the Council notices change over time, with the review promises getting longer?
- How many rumours should be live at once? The draft suggests five, rotating through the day.
