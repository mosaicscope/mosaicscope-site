# Lore notes 6: glossary and continuity

This is the reference for everyone writing lore for Mosaicscope. It lists the names, places and terms that have appeared so far, the timeline the story runs on, and the continuity rules that keep a dozen writers from contradicting each other. When a note in the other documents disagrees with this one, this one wins, and the other note should be fixed.

## Glossary: people

**Tiles.** The main guy of the Mosaics. Throws the parties. Home: the Plaza.
**Glitter.** Tiles' girl. Gossip and records. Home: the Stage (where she starts the rumours), with the market as her daytime place.
**Kodak.** The photographer. Home: the Studio.
**Nova.** The calm one. First to help. Home: the Studio.
**Grain.** The hype man with a short fuse. Pranks. Home: the Stage.
**Flash.** Quiet, holds grudges. Home: the playground.
**Ruby.** The alley tinkerer. Trades everything. Home: the alley.
**Bando.** Competitive, runs the court. Home: the court.
**Lux.** Curious, makes friends fast. Home: the park.
**Static.** The DJ. Guards the boombox. Home: the Stage.
**Dr Quakenstein.** A scientist. The freeze. Tagged `villain` in the packs.
**Bryce.** The self-employed superhero. The signal. Rides a Lime scooter.
**Kevin.** The giraffe at the Velvet Zoo. Eats paper and hats.
**The Don.** Head of the Famiglia. Mostly heard about, rarely seen.
**Gina.** The Famiglia's ledger keeper. Writes in pen.
**Sal.** The mince pies at Two Pies. The interest.
**Nico.** The Famiglia's weakest threat.
**Vito.** The Don's car and garage.
**Mike.** The Famiglia's smuggler. Gnomes and sneakers.
**Terrance.** The live feed.
**Wilf.** The satirist of bureaucracy.
**The Riccarton Boys.** Hubcap sellers on Moorhouse.
**The Council's clerk.** Unnamed. The person placing the cones. Introduced only in Act 3.

## Glossary: places

**The Plaza.** The main square, next to the Cathedral.
**Cathedral Square.** Older and bigger. Best view of the signal. Home to the Kevin mural.
**The Avon.** The river. The spine of the city.
**The Bridge of Remembrance.** The crossing the Council wants to protect. The cones live here.
**The Court.** Bando's court.
**The Stage.** The music venue and the late-night spot.
**The Studio.** The quiet creative space, where people shelter in the rain.
**The Park and the Velvet Zoo.** Green space, with Kevin.
**The Alley.** Ruby's back streets and trades.
**The Playground.** Flash's place.
**Two Pies.** The mince-pie shop. The Famiglia's front.
**The casino.** The pokies and the money gun. The Famiglia's main business.
**Moorhouse.** The strip of car yards and hubcap sellers.
**The Port Hills.** The high ground to the south. Where the signal goes up.

## Glossary: terms

**The signal.** Bryce's kiwi-signal over the Port Hills. A shared hope, and sometimes a warning.
**The cones.** Road cones on the Bridge of Remembrance. Part of the Council's ransom. The pattern changes.
**The freeze.** Quakenstein's plan to freeze the Avon. Began as a jar at a science fair.
**The ledger.** Gina's pen record of the Famiglia's debts. Nine names and one blank line.
**The tab.** The debt to the Famiglia. Paid in pies, which are the interest.
**The Pink Pukeko.** A car that has been for sale for years. The city's running joke.
**Moon Sherbet.** A glowing substance that turns up in pockets. Stolen from the Famiglia.
**Mince Debt.** The hospital's diagnosis for Sal's pie habit. It is a joke, and the hospital is serious about it.
**Run it back.** Tiles' catchphrase.
**Get my good side.** Glitter's catchphrase.

## The timeline

The story runs over a fixed number of in-game days, but the day is only a loose guide. The events below are fixed in order and loosely fixed in time.

**Before the game.** The jar at the science fair. The first cone on the bridge after a meeting that didn't fix the pothole. The Famiglia's first loan, which nobody remembers.

**Day one.** The cones are moved for the first time in the game. The freeze is first mentioned by the busker. The signal goes up over the Port Hills on the first night.

**Days two to four.** The gossip spreads. Terrance posts the first ice warning. The Council announces a review. The Pink Pukeko is listed again. Kevin eats the first paper.

**Days five to seven.** The pieces appear. The ledger is seen by Ruby. The garage's car is scratched. The power fails at the Stage for the first time.

**Day eight onward.** The answer becomes reachable. The ledger's blank line can be read. The clerk can be seen at dawn. The tab can close.

Writers should assume a player can take any amount of time, so the timeline should not be relied on to unlock anything. It is a guide to what is plausible, not a schedule.

## Continuity rules

1. **Names are fixed.** Use the names in the glossary exactly. Never give a Mosaic a second name or a surname.
2. **Places are fixed.** A place keeps its feel. The Stage is loud, the Studio is quiet, and the Alley is never clean.
3. **The signal goes up at night.** Never in daylight, and never for a reason the city agrees on.
4. **The cones move at dawn, or are found at dawn.** Never in the afternoon.
5. **The ledger is in pen.** Anyone who mentions the ledger should mention the pen.
6. **Kevin eats paper and hats.** Nothing else, unless a beat is specifically about it.
7. **Quakenstein never has authority over the cones.** He can describe them, not control them.
8. **Bryce is not a cat person, but he takes lost cats seriously.** Both are true.
9. **The Don is not seen in person before Act 3.** Only heard about, or seen from a distance.
10. **Sal's pies are not the money.** They are the interest on the money, and the interest is the joke.
11. **The feed is never wrong about time.** It can be wrong about what happened.
12. **Rumours never resolve in the game.** The player may believe one, but the game does not confirm it.

## Things that need a decision before release

- The exact number of in-game days the story takes.
- Whether Glitter is at the Stage or the market in the daytime. The glossary says both, and that is a conflict to settle.
- Whether the clerk ever gets a name. The current draft says no.
- Whether the Kevin mural is ever explained. The draft says no.
- Whether Terrance is a person or a service. The draft says both, which is deliberate, but the writers should agree.

## How to add to this document

When a writer adds a new name, place or term, they should add it here first, with a one-line description, before using it in any exchange. If a name is used in an exchange before it's in the glossary, that is a continuity error, and the merge check should flag it.
