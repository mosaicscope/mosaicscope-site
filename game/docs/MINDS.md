# NPC minds

Each simulated person has a mind built on named psychology models (see the comments in `src/mind/`):
Big Five personality, core affect with appraisal (Russell; Lazarus; OCC), Maslow / self-determination needs,
temporal-difference value learning, Garcia one-trial taste aversion, prospect-theory loss aversion,
Ebbinghaus forgetting with emotional boosting, habit formation by context cues (Wood & Neal), social learning
(Bandura) and Lazarus & Folkman coping. A regional culture layer (Aotearoa New Zealand / Canterbury / Christchurch,
from published cross-cultural research such as Hofstede's dimensions) shapes trait distributions, norms, dress and
dialogue tone. The numbers are published, approximate figures encoded by hand, not scraped data.

The result is a terrarium: tastes, fears, habits and routines form from lived experience and spread socially, and
the player's actions leave lasting marks.
