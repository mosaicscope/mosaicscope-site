# Christchurch Chaos

Open **Life phone** in the top bar (inside Menu on mobile). The earlier Explore,
Betting, Racing, Farm and UC screens remain available from the phone and top bar.
All money and gambling are fictional game money. University progression is fictional.

## Twenty world and progression upgrades

1. A phone hub links activities, banking, jobs, study, map and the saved town journal.
2. An interactive map shows destinations, your position and encounter locations; select a destination to walk there.
3. Nearby exploration scenes have world-space props, witnesses and a labelled marker.
4. Completed encounters schedule a one-time callback with a neighbour, cash and relationship consequences.
5. A saved calendar tracks elections, races, assignments, hearings and festivals; daily ticks handle bills and income.
6. A separate 4×4 farm screen has an avatar, adjacent movement and a drivable tractor.
7. Individual animals have editable names, parentage, age, health, temperament, handling and breeding cooldowns.
8. Tractor attachments perform the work appropriate to the field under the tractor.
9. Wheat, corn and potatoes have different growth times and prices; soil, moisture and weeds affect the harvest. Irrigation, fertiliser and spray improve those inputs.
10. Tractor contracts require equipment; hired workers improve contract income and provide stock care for daily wages.
11. Horse training uses four ordered track gates, with fitness use and a cooldown between sessions.
12. Three named rival trainers have saved relationships and exchange limited training advice.
13. Four scheduled meets form a racing season, with rival standings, championship prizes and trophies.
14. The animated casino floor has named dealers and a saved three-round high-card tournament.
15. Craps points, Crash flights, reserved race stakes, results and tournaments belong to each save slot. Race payouts settle once on the simulation clock.
16. A garage shows component condition and prices repairs; purchased parts are saved.
17. Towing moves the owned car to a garage. Insurance charges daily premiums; claims enforce damage, excess and daily limits.
18. A separate UC campus screen has rooms, library assignment submission, a club, cafe, career office and access to courses/graduation.
19. Eight degree subjects unlock matching career interviews; shifts pay wages and earn promotions. Re-interviewing cannot bypass shift cooldowns.
20. Recurring NPC profiles connect their existing role, ambitions, daily story chapters and relationship history to the phone journal.

## Twenty stranger upgrades

1. Campaign for mayor, contest a scheduled election and enact funded city policies.
2. Buy eligible real town properties, change their labels and collect daily business income.
3. Spread temporary conspiracies that reduce nearby NPC trust and appear in speech bubbles.
4. A persistent named nemesis gains feud heat, sabotages deals and can accept peace offerings.
5. Build a mobility scooter and enter the pensioner derby; parts improve its chances.
6. Turn individually named farm animals into celebrities with appearance fees and daily fame income.
7. Name a movement, gain followers and support, and provoke schisms as it grows.
8. Sign a reality-TV contract; timed episodes pay fees, with feuds changing fame growth.
9. An unpermitted chicken palace adds feed storage but triggers council heat and hearings. A permit resolves it.
10. Fit limousine/hearse bodies, a tractor engine, double deck, tyres and neon. These alter silhouette, colour, grip, mass or speed, subject to a four-part limit.
11. Prepare heirs and a family trust; after seven story days, inherit the businesses, savings and surviving feud as the next generation.
12. Organise a festival with paid acts and toilets; preparation changes ticket returns, reputation, TV fame and council trouble.
13. Enrol your horse in Commerce, pass three easy timed courses and earn championship bonuses.
14. Open a pigeon hotel, drive-through weddings, support tractor hire or breakup photography business.
15. Compete for four town factions; saved influence earns cash and political support.
16. File a ridiculous lawsuit, gather timed evidence and attend court; winning creates a permanent precedent and cools the feud.
17. Connect owned properties with tunnels; two connections enable travel. Rain causes flooding without a pump.
18. Inspect a property for ghosts; restoring their wallpaper turns tenant refunds into friendly-haunting income.
19. Start a four-stage bull/bus/wedding/campaign disaster chain with delayed, connected consequences.
20. Mayor monuments, renamed buildings and disaster landmarks remain in the save and on the map.

These are single-player systems. The farm and campus are separate interactive screens;
there are no new buildings or a regenerated town. Scooter races, court hearings,
politics and NPC life chapters use management/story mechanics rather than new 3D scenes.
Existing save slots remain version 6; missing life and casino state receive defaults.
