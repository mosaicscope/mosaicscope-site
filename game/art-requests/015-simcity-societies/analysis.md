# Analysis: SimCity Societies street

What is in the picture:
- Tenements and houses packed close, every window with a life: curtains, plants, figures at the glass.
- The "soul" is in small domestic scenes, not in building shape.

Integration, reworked for Mosaics' own heart (a Christchurch parody with stick-figure charm):
- Behind about a third of lit windows there is a household story, drawn as the game's own stick-figure silhouettes: someone at the glass, a couple, a television flickering blue with the back of a head on the couch, curtains half drawn, a cat on the sill, a pot plant, a standard lamp.
- Stories are a pure hash of the window (deterministic; nothing saved; sim untouched), shown from zoom 1.1 so zoomed-out views stay clean.
