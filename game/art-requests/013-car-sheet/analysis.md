# Analysis: car sheet

What is in the picture:
- Dozens of small hatchbacks and sedans, no two the same: different colours, two-tone roofs, stripes, stickers, spoilers, aerials.
- Headlights differ: some white, some yellowed.
- Dice and hangers, roof paint a different colour from the body.

Integration (CarPatina.ts):
- Two-tone and primer roofs, sun-fade and per-car paint tint so a row of "white" cars is never one white.
- Stickers, pinstripes (a stripe along the sill), whip aerials, yellowed headlamps on older cars, missing hubcaps, bigger warmer headlight pools at night.
- Service vehicles (police, ambulance, fire, post, bus, taxi) stay smart: no rust, stickers or primer.
- Original art only; the sheet informed the variety, not the shapes.
