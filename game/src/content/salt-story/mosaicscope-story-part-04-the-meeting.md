# MosaicScope Story, Part 4: The Meeting

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1: "The Cold Snap." Part 2: "The Nor'wester." Part 3: "The Morning After."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 3:** Ruth has agreed to meet Vito at the Market and told him to bring his own lollies. Jace's decoy ute plan has Kyle worried. Velvet has bought a gold painting of Vito's Roma tomato for the gala, and Vito doesn't know yet. The Night Kiwi says the dairy is about to be the most interesting place in the SALT.

---

## Scene 1: The Market, Early Evening

*The Market is winding down. Stalls are packing up, the smell of pav and frying onions hangs in the air. A single plastic table sits in the middle of the car park, with two folding chairs and a tin of biscuits. Ruth arrives first, carrying a canvas bag. Vito's black car pulls in beside her, precisely parked. Sal gets out of the passenger side carrying a small wooden box.*

**Ruth:** You're early, Vito. Did you think I wouldn't show?

**Vito:** I thought you'd be late, Ruth. Women are always late to important meetings. It's the lipstick.

**Ruth:** I'm not wearing lipstick, bro.

**Vito:** Then it's a different kind of lateness. Sit, sit. Sal, the chairs.

**Sal:** I've got the chairs, Don.

**Vito:** Then sit them down, Sal. Don't stand there like a broken fridge.

*Sal sets out the chairs, then opens the wooden box with great ceremony. Inside, nestled in straw, sit twelve pineapple lollies and a single tomato, wrapped in tissue paper.*

**Ruth:** Is that the tomato?

**Vito:** That's the tomato. A Roma. My best one. I brought it as a gift.

**Ruth:** You're giving me your best tomato, Vito?

**Vito:** I'm giving you my respect, Ruth. The tomato is just how I express it.

**Ruth:** Okay. Well, I brought biscuits.

**Vito:** Biscuits? In a tin? How very sensible.

**Ruth:** It's a dairy tin, bro. It holds everything.

*Vito takes a biscuit. He bites into it. Long pause.*

**Vito:** Hm. Stale.

**Ruth:** It's a dairy biscuit, Vito. It's been in the tin since the Christchurch earthquake.

**Vito:** Then it's a very historic biscuit. I respect that.

*Sal leans in and whispers to Vito. Vito waves him away.*

**Vito:** So, Ruth. The Riccarton Boys are a problem.

**Ruth:** They're a problem for both of us, Vito. They've been parking across my bins and doing burnouts on my pavement.

**Vito:** They've been doing burnouts on my pavement too, Ruth. In front of the casino. Tony nearly had a heart attack.

**Ruth:** Tony's always having a heart attack.

**Vito:** That's his hobby. Let him have it.

**Ruth:** Okay, Vito. Here's my offer. I'll pay the lollies, but I want the Riccarton Boys off my street. And I want the protection rate cut in half.

**Vito:** Half? Ruth, you're asking for the moon. The moon is expensive.

**Ruth:** The moon's not in the SALT, Vito. It's in the sky. And it's free.

**Vito:** Ha! Very clever. Sal, did you hear that? She's clever.

**Sal:** I heard it, Don. She's always clever when she's angry.

**Vito:** Then she's always angry, Sal. Let's keep her happy.

*Ruth picks up a lolly from the box and turns it over in her fingers.*

**Ruth:** Vito, I'll be honest with you. I'm tired. The council's on my back about the pizza sign. The Riccarton Boys are on my pavement. And I'm running out of biscuits.

**Vito:** Ah, Ruth. Life is hard for the small business owner. That's why we have the Famiglia. We take care of the hard parts.

**Ruth:** You take care of the lollies, Vito. You don't take care of anything else.

**Vito:** Ruth. The lollies are a symbol. Of friendship. Of security. Of pineapple.

**Ruth:** Pineapple's not a symbol, bro. It's a fruit that's been on pizza for too long.

*Gurjit comes walking over, wrapped in his tea towel, carrying a box of pizzas.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth! I brought pizza!

**Vito:** Gurjit! My friend! Pineapple?

**Gurjit:** Always, Vito. It's my identity.

**Vito:** A man who knows what he wants. I respect that.

**Gurjit:** Thank you, Vito. Can I keep my sign?

**Vito:** Your sign? Ah. The council's been talking to you, Gurjit.

**Gurjit:** They say it's a hazard.

**Vito:** It's not a hazard, Gurjit. It's a beacon. A pizza beacon. The whole SALT can see it.

**Gurjit:** Thank you, Vito!

**Ruth:** Gurjit, sit down. You're making this worse.

**Gurjit:** I'm making it better, Ruth. Look at Vito. He's smiling.

**Ruth:** That's not a smile, bro. That's a warning.

## Scene 2: Riccarton Road, Ten Minutes Later

*Dusk is falling. Jace is parked at the kerb near the Market in a battered ute, engine running, bass turned low. Kyle stands beside it, arms folded, looking worried. Tanner and Brooke are sitting on the bonnet, eating hokey pokey.*

**Kyle:** Jace, I'm not happy about this, mate.

**Jace:** You're never happy, Kyle. That's your brand.

**Kyle:** My brand is fixing cars that break because of idiots like you.

**Jace:** Thanks, Kyle. Great brand.

**Tanner:** Jace, I think the decoy's working.

**Jace:** Of course it's working, Tanner. It's got sick subwoofers and a giraffe sticker.

**Brooke:** That's Kevin's sticker, Jace. From the Velvet gala.

**Jace:** Yeah, well, Kevin's a legend, bro. He's the best sticker in town.

*In the distance, a police siren wails. Blue and red lights flicker at the far end of the road.*

**Tanner:** Oh no. It's Pip.

**Jace:** Right. Brooke, Tanner, get in the ute. Kyle, you're on the bonnet. Look like you're fixing it.

**Kyle:** I'm not fixing it, Jace. I've never fixed it.

**Jace:** Then look like you're trying.

*Pip's patrol car rolls to a stop beside the decoy ute. She leans out of the window, torch in hand. She shines it straight at Kyle.*

**Pip:** Evening, Kyle.

**Kyle:** Evening, Constable. Just checking the engine.

**Pip:** Is it making a noise like a sad goose?

**Kyle:** Yeah. Very sad.

**Pip:** I can hear it from here. Is that the Riccarton ute?

**Kyle:** Nah. It's Jace's. He's borrowed it.

**Pip:** Did he borrow it or did he steal it?

**Kyle:** Borrowed, officer. Definitely borrowed.

*Pip shines the torch into the back of the ute. Tanner and Brooke are crouched down, trying to look like hubcaps.*

**Pip:** Tanner. Brooke. Why are you in the back of a ute?

**Tanner:** Hubcap inspection, officer.

**Brooke:** We're checking the hubs for safety.

**Pip:** The hubs are on the car, Brooke. You're in the back.

**Brooke:** They're very safe hubs, officer.

*Pip pulls out a notebook and starts writing. Jace, in the driver's seat of the real hatchback two cars down, pulls out quietly and drives off.*

**Pip:** Kyle, where's Jace?

**Kyle:** Dunno, officer. He's not my problem.

**Pip:** Then he's my problem.

*Pip looks up. The real yellow hatchback is roaring away down Riccarton Road, lights off, bass shaking the street.*

**Pip:** Oh, you're kidding me.

**Kyle:** I told him it'd fall apart if he drove it loud, officer.

**Pip:** Get in the car, Kyle. We're going after the decoy.

**Kyle:** Which decoy?

**Pip:** The one that's not the decoy, Kyle. Keep up.

*Pip's car peels away. Kyle scrambles into the passenger seat, clutching the rag he's been using all evening.*

**Kyle:** Pip, honest, I'm really bad at car chases.

**Pip:** Then hold on to your rag, Kyle.

## Scene 3: The Market Car Park, Night

*The car park is almost empty. The table is still there, now lit by a single lamp. Ruth and Vito are still talking. Sal has his back to them, watching the road. Gurjit has gone. The pizza box is empty.*

**Vito:** Ruth. I like you. You're not afraid of me.

**Ruth:** I'm a bit afraid of you, Vito. You've got a very serious face.

**Vito:** It's the face of a man who loves tomatoes.

**Ruth:** Okay. I'll take the deal. Half the rate. The Riccarton Boys off my street. And you keep your tomato in your box.

**Vito:** Ah. The tomato. Ruth, I have to tell you something.

**Ruth:** Oh no. What is it?

**Vito:** The Velvet gala. Tonight. Sir Ponsonby has bought a painting.

**Ruth:** A painting of what?

**Vito:** Of my tomato.

**Ruth:** The one in the box?

**Vito:** No, Ruth. A gold one. Painted by a very expensive artist in a very expensive studio. He says it's a tribute.

**Ruth:** Vito, that's not a tribute. That's a theft.

**Vito:** It's a theft of a picture, Ruth. The tomato is still mine. But the picture is his. And he's going to show it to the whole city tonight.

**Ruth:** So you're going to the gala?

**Vito:** I'm going to the gala with my lollies, Ruth. And I'm going to take back my tomato.

**Ruth:** You're going to steal the painting?

**Vito:** I'm going to *borrow* the painting. Politely. With Sal.

**Sal:** I'm not stealing a painting, Don.

**Vito:** You're not stealing it, Sal. You're carrying it.

**Sal:** That's still stealing, Don.

**Vito:** Then you're a very good thief, Sal. Your nonna would be proud.

*Ruth stands up slowly, looking at the lollies, then at Vito.*

**Ruth:** You know what? I'm coming to the gala.

**Vito:** You're not invited, Ruth.

**Ruth:** Priya's invited. And she's got a bow tie. I'm going to borrow it.

**Vito:** That's not how it works, Ruth.

**Ruth:** It's exactly how it works, Vito. Ask Kevin.

## Scene 4: Velvet Mansion, Night of the Gala

*Velvet Mansion has been transformed. Fairy lights, a string quartet, a red carpet, and a sea of people in shiny clothes. Mikey Stone holds the velvet rope with grim concentration. Saffron glides past the crowd, a champagne glass in one hand and a phone in the other. Priya stands at the entrance in a bow tie that's far too big for her. Kevin the giraffe wears a bow tie of his own, slightly crooked.*

**Mikey:** Name?

**Priya:** Priya. I'm on the list, I think.

**Mikey:** Priya. Yeah. You're on the list. Bow tie's a good look, love.

**Priya:** It's Kevin's.

**Mikey:** Kevin's got good taste. Go on in.

**Priya:** Thanks, Mikey.

**Mikey:** And mind the tomato, love. It's been having a moment.

**Priya:** What tomato?

**Mikey:** The gold one. On the wall. It's been glaring at people since six.

*Inside, the crowd murmurs. Priya walks through, notebook in hand. She stops in front of a large gold-framed painting on the far wall: a single, glowing Roma tomato, rendered in shimmering gold leaf. A small brass plaque reads "Velvet's Tribute to the Roma."*

**Priya:** Oh my god.

**Saffron:** It's a lot, isn't it?

**Priya:** It's enormous.

**Saffron:** It's the most expensive tomato in Canterbury, love. Velvet's very proud.

**Priya:** Is it real?

**Saffron:** It's a painting, babe. Nothing real about it. Except the price.

*Sir Ponsonby Velvet appears at the top of the staircase, arms outstretched, champagne in one hand.*

**Velvet:** Ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to the Velvet gala! Tonight we celebrate art, friendship, and the finest tomato in the SALT!

*Polite applause. A few people look at each other, puzzled.*

**Velvet:** And, of course, a special guest. The Don himself, Vittorio Tessera!

*Silence. Then the doors at the back open. Vito walks in, wearing a pinstripe suit, a lolly in one hand, Sal behind him carrying a small, empty wooden box.*

**Vito:** Ponsonby. You've been busy.

**Velvet:** Vittorio! My friend! I've got a gift for you.

**Vito:** A gift? Ponsonby, you've put my tomato on your wall.

**Velvet:** It's a tribute, Vittorio. Everyone loves a tribute.

**Vito:** I don't like tributes, Ponsonby. I like tomatoes. And I like them in my garden, where they belong.

**Velvet:** But the painting's so beautiful, Vittorio. It's the most beautiful tomato in the world.

**Vito:** It's a painting. It doesn't water itself, Ponsonby.

*A loud crash from the back of the room. Everyone turns. Glitter has knocked over a champagne tower. She's standing in the wreckage, looking horrified.*

**Glitter:** Oops! Sorry, everyone! Just a bit of a spill!

**Velvet:** Glitter! You're not on the list either!

**Glitter:** I'm on the list of people who bring the fun, Ponsonby. It's a much bigger list.

*Tiles bursts through the front doors, wearing a shiny gold jacket and a bow tie that's been taken off someone else. Flash and Ruby follow. Honey marches in last, clipboard under one arm.*

**Tiles:** Party's here! Who's ready for a painting heist?

**Flash:** I'm ready, bro! I've got a foam sword!

**Ruby:** Flash, you're not bringing a foam sword to a gala.

**Flash:** I'm bringing it to the painting, Ruby. The painting needs a sword.

**Honey:** The painting's on the list, Flash. Red zone.

**Tiles:** Honey, I'm not in the red zone tonight. I'm in the gold zone.

**Honey:** That's not a zone, Tiles.

**Tiles:** It is now, Honey. It's a gold zone. Stop arguing.

*Vito looks at Tiles, then at Sal, then at the gold tomato on the wall. He sighs deeply, then slowly lifts his lolly to his mouth.*

**Vito:** Sal.

**Sal:** Don?

**Vito:** Get the box.

**Sal:** Which box, Don?

**Vito:** The one that's going to be full of tomatoes by the end of the night.

*The lights dim. The string quartet hits a dramatic chord. Everyone in the room freezes. From high above, on the mansion's gallery, a dark shape sits perfectly still, watching.*

**Night Kiwi (off-screen, dry and dramatic):** Tonight, the SALT takes its painting back.

## End of Part 4

**Story threads moved forward:**

- Ruth has struck a deal with Vito: half the protection rate, the Riccarton Boys off her street, and a tomato left in its box.
- Jace has escaped in the real hatchback, and Pip and Kyle are now chasing the decoy into the night.
- Vito has crashed the Velvet gala with Sal and a box, intending to "borrow" the gold painting of his tomato.
- Priya is inside the gala wearing Kevin's bow tie and holding a notebook.
- Glitter wrecked a champagne tower. Tiles, Flash, Ruby and Honey have arrived to join the heist.
- The Night Kiwi is watching from the gallery.
