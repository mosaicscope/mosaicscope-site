# MosaicScope Story, Part 8: The Station

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1: "The Cold Snap." Part 2: "The Nor'wester." Part 3: "The Morning After." Part 4: "The Meeting." Part 5: "The Heist." Part 6: "The Revenge." Part 7: "The Raid."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 7:** The Riccarton Boys ended the dairy raid in a biscuit truce. Pip has told Jace, Tanner and Brooke to come to the station with a biscuit. Vito has the empty biscuit tin. Pip is holding the bin lid as evidence.

---

## Scene 1: Central Police Station, Morning

*The front counter of the Central Police Station. A bell on a spring. A noticeboard with a dozen flyers: a lost cat called Biscuit, a yoga class, and a poster that reads "PARKING: IT'S NOT A RIGHT, IT'S A RESPONSIBILITY." A tomato sits on the counter, in a small glass jar with a handwritten label: "Roma, Do Not Water." Hobbs sits behind the desk, eating a cheese scone. Pip stands beside him, clipboard in hand.*

**Hobbs:** Pip, why is there a tomato on my counter?

**Pip:** It's evidence, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** Evidence of what?

**Pip:** Of a very confusing week, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** Right. And the bin lid?

**Pip:** That's in the back room, next to the Night Kiwi's hat.

**Hobbs:** Pip, why do we have the Night Kiwi's hat?

**Pip:** Kyle left it on the bench, Sarge. He said it wasn't his.

**Hobbs:** Whose is it, then?

**Pip:** He reckons it belongs to a rumour, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** You can't book a rumour, Pip.

**Pip:** I know, Sarge. But I can put it in a box.

*The bell on the counter rings. Jace walks in, cap pulled low, hands in his pockets. Tanner and Brooke follow, looking sheepish. Each of them is holding a biscuit in a paper napkin.*

**Jace:** Morning, Sarge. We've brought the biscuits.

**Hobbs:** Brilliant. Put them on the counter, mate. Next to the tomato.

**Tanner:** Is it a tomato, Sarge?

**Hobbs:** It's a Roma, Tanner. Don't touch it. Vito's very particular.

**Brooke:** Sarge, can we have a receipt for the biscuits?

**Hobbs:** Brooke, nobody gets a receipt for a biscuit.

**Brooke:** Then how do we prove we brought them?

**Hobbs:** You prove it by being here, Brooke. Sit down.

*The three of them sit on the plastic chairs along the wall. Jace stares at the wall. Tanner examines his biscuit. Brooke examines Tanner's biscuit.*

**Tanner:** Brooke, that's my biscuit.

**Brooke:** It's the same biscuit, Tanner. The biscuit's a community resource.

**Hobbs:** Right. Let's get on with it. Jace, you're first. Explain yourself.

**Jace:** Sarge, I'm not sure where to start.

**Hobbs:** Start with the hubcaps.

**Jace:** The hubcaps were a fundraiser, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** For who?

**Jace:** For the Riccarton Boys, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** And who runs the Riccarton Boys?

**Jace:** Me, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** And who runs the fundraiser?

**Jace:** Me again, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** So you've been fundraising from yourself.

**Jace:** It's a very efficient charity, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** Jace, I'm writing this down.

**Jace:** You don't have a pen, Sarge. You told Pip your pen was broken.

**Hobbs:** I've got a backup pen, Jace. It's in my scone.

## Scene 2: Interview Room Two, Same Morning

*A small room with a table, two chairs, and a long mirror on one wall. Pip sits opposite Tanner. Brooke stands in the corner, eating the last of his biscuit. Honey sits in the observer's chair in the corner, clipboard on her lap.*

**Pip:** Tanner. Tell me about the parking.

**Tanner:** Officer, I'm not sure I understand the question.

**Pip:** The SALT parking. Why do you want it?

**Tanner:** Because it's close to the Market, officer. And the Market has the best pav.

**Pip:** That's not a reason to take a car park, Tanner.

**Tanner:** It's a very good reason, officer. Ask anyone.

**Pip:** I'm asking you.

**Tanner:** Then the answer's yes, officer. It's a very good reason.

**Honey (from the corner):** Officer, can I make a note?

**Pip:** Honey, you're not supposed to be in here.

**Honey:** I'm the observer, officer. I'm observing.

**Pip:** Observe quietly, then.

**Honey:** I'm very quiet, officer. Just colour-coded.

*Brooke steps forward, holding up his biscuit like a microphone.*

**Brooke:** Officer, can I say something?

**Pip:** Go on, Brooke.

**Brooke:** I think the parking's a symbol, officer. For freedom. For being a young bloke in a town that's always under renovation.

**Pip:** Brooke, that's very poetic.

**Brooke:** Thanks, officer. I'm a bit of a poet.

**Tanner:** He's not a poet, officer. He's a tracksuit enthusiast.

**Brooke:** Tanner, shut up.

**Tanner:** You shut up, Brooke. You've got a stripe missing.

**Brooke:** It's a design choice, Tanner!

**Pip:** Enough. Both of you, sit down.

*Brooke and Tanner sit. Pip sighs and flips her notebook shut.*

**Pip:** Here's what's going to happen. You're each getting a warning, and a fine for the burnout. And you're going to pay for the bins.

**Tanner:** What about the biscuits, officer?

**Pip:** The biscuits are on the house, Tanner. Ruth insisted.

**Honey (from the corner):** Ruth also said the biscuits are historic.

**Pip:** Honey, that's enough.

**Honey:** Sorry, officer. I'm just adding it to the list.

## Scene 3: Hobbs' Break, Midday

*The break room of the station. A kettle, a fridge with a sticky note reading "NOT THE SARGE'S MILK", and a small pot plant on the windowsill. Hobbs is sitting by the window with a tea. Pip walks in holding the Night Kiwi's hat in a clear plastic bag, and sits down opposite him.*

**Hobbs:** Pip, you've got a hat in a bag.

**Pip:** It's the Night Kiwi's hat, Sarge. Kyle left it on the bench.

**Hobbs:** I told you, Pip. You can't book a rumour.

**Pip:** I'm not booking it, Sarge. I'm investigating it.

**Hobbs:** Investigating a hat?

**Pip:** Investigating the person who wears it, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** And who's that?

**Pip:** Nobody knows, Sarge. That's the mystery.

**Hobbs:** Pip, I've been on this beat for twenty-six years. There's always a mystery. It's called Tuesday.

**Pip:** This one's different, Sarge. I've seen him on a roof. I've seen him in the dairy. I've seen him in the Velvet back room.

**Hobbs:** You've been in the Velvet back room?

**Pip:** No, Sarge. But I've seen the Velvet back room from the roof.

**Hobbs:** Pip, you've been on the roof?

**Pip:** I've been on the roof, Sarge. It's got a very nice view.

**Hobbs:** Right. Well, if you find the Night Kiwi, bring me a pie.

**Pip:** Why, Sarge?

**Hobbs:** Because he's obviously eating them. Somebody's been stealing pies from the Burger Joint.

**Pip:** Sarge, the Night Kiwi's not a pie thief.

**Hobbs:** Pip, everyone's a pie thief if the pie is good enough.

*The kettle clicks off. Hobbs gets up and pours a second tea, without asking, and places it in front of Pip.*

**Hobbs:** Here. Drink this. You look like you've been chasing ducks.

**Pip:** I have been chasing ducks, Sarge. Kyle drove a decoy into the pond.

**Hobbs:** Right. Put that in the report.

**Pip:** Under what heading, Sarge?

**Hobbs:** "Ducks, decoys, Night Kiwi's hat. Probably not important."

**Pip:** That's the same heading as last time, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** It's a good heading, Pip. It covers everything.

## Scene 4: The Market, Afternoon

*The Market has a new stall. A hand-painted sign reads "NIGHT KIWI'S PIES: NO QUESTIONS ASKED." Behind it, Bill is sitting on a folding chair with a tray of sausage rolls, next to Priya, who is taking notes on her laptop.*

**Priya:** Bill, did you set up this stall?

**Bill:** I did, love. I'm a businessman now.

**Priya:** Since when?

**Bill:** Since the Burger Joint closed the pie counter because someone nicked a tray of them.

**Priya:** Who nicked them?

**Bill:** Nobody knows, love. That's why it's called a mystery.

**Priya:** Bill, I think you're the Night Kiwi.

**Bill:** Ha! I'm too old to climb roofs, love. My hips won't allow it.

**Priya:** Then who is it?

**Bill:** It's a rumour, love. And rumours don't eat pies.

**Priya:** They do if they're hungry.

**Bill:** That's a very good line, love. Put it in your essay.

*Mo the busker walks past with his guitar, stopping to look at the sign.*

**Mo:** Bill, you're selling pies under a rumour's name?

**Bill:** It's good marketing, Mo. Everyone wants to be a rumour.

**Mo:** Everyone wants to be a legend, Bill. Rumours are just legends who haven't been caught.

**Bill:** That's a lovely line, Mo. Want a sausage roll?

**Mo:** I'll take one for the song.

**Bill:** What song?

**Mo:** One about a bloke who sells pies and never gets caught. Sixteen verses.

**Bill:** Sixteen verses? For a pie?

**Mo:** It's a very long pie, Bill.

*Priya stops typing and looks up. Across the Market car park, a figure in a dark jacket walks past a stall, pausing at the sausage rolls. The figure has a kiwi-bird mask on, though the face is turned away.*

**Priya:** Bill. Look.

**Bill:** That's the Night Kiwi, love. Keep your eyes on the pies.

**Priya:** He's taking a sausage roll.

**Bill:** He's paying for it, love. Look.

*The figure drops a coin into a tin on the stall, takes a sausage roll, and walks on without looking back. A moment later he's gone around a corner.*

**Priya:** That's the most polite thing I've ever seen a rumour do.

**Bill:** That's because he's a rumour with manners, love.

## Scene 5: Riccarton Motors, Late Afternoon

*The showroom. Dust on the windscreens. A sign in the window reads "RICCARTON MOTO" in red, still flickering. Mr Dwyer stands in front of a row of cars with no hubcaps. Kyle leans on the bonnet of an old ute, arms crossed.*

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle. How many hubcaps?

**Kyle:** Four, boss. All stolen.

**Mr Dwyer:** Four hubcaps. And where are they?

**Kyle:** One's in the bin, boss. One's in the dairy. One's on a bloke called Tanner's bonnet. And one's in the duck pond.

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle, the duck pond's in the Park.

**Kyle:** I know, boss. The decoy's in there too.

**Mr Dwyer:** What decoy?

**Kyle:** It's a long story, boss. Involving Jace and a giraffe sticker.

**Mr Dwyer:** Jace is fired.

**Kyle:** He's already fired, boss. He's been fired since Tuesday.

**Mr Dwyer:** Then why's he still here?

**Kyle:** Because he's Jace, boss. He doesn't leave. He just sits on traffic cones.

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle, I want those hubcaps back. All four. By Friday.

**Kyle:** That's a tall order, boss. The bin's a long way from the duck pond.

**Mr Dwyer:** Then get a ute, Kyle. And a bloke who can swim.

**Kyle:** I know a bloke who can swim, boss. He's got a pair of togs and a fear of ducks.

**Mr Dwyer:** Good. Get him.

*Mr Dwyer turns to go, then stops and looks back at the sign.*

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle. Turn the sign off, would you? It's giving me a headache.

**Kyle:** Can't, boss. It's been flickering since Tuesday.

**Mr Dwyer:** Since Tuesday?

**Kyle:** Yeah, boss. Since the Nor'wester. Something about the wiring.

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle, the sign's been flickering for a week and nobody's fixed it?

**Kyle:** It's a very stubborn sign, boss. Like a goose.

**Mr Dwyer:** Kyle, you're fired too.

**Kyle:** That's fair, boss. I'll get my bag.

## Scene 6: The Dairy, Evening

*The dairy is quiet. The neon sign hums. Ruth is wiping down the counter. Gurjit is on his ladder again, straightening the pizza sign. Sal stands in the doorway, holding the empty biscuit tin wrapped in a tea towel, like a museum piece.*

**Sal:** Ruth, the Don wants you to have this back.

**Ruth:** Sal, that's my biscuit tin.

**Sal:** It's the Don's biscuit tin now, Ruth. He's had it on his mantelpiece since Thursday.

**Ruth:** On the mantelpiece?

**Sal:** Yes, Ruth. Next to the painting of the tomato. He says they're friends now.

**Ruth:** The tomato and the tin?

**Sal:** The tomato and the tin, Ruth. And the lolly. Velvet sent a card.

**Ruth:** What did the card say?

**Sal:** It said, "Thank you for the lolly, Vittorio. The painting is coming down on Friday. I'm donating it to the Market. The tomato is free to go home."

**Ruth:** Good on Velvet.

**Sal:** Yeah. And the Don says he's going to give the tomato to the Market too. For the pav stall.

**Ruth:** The Roma's going into the pav?

**Sal:** The Roma's going into the pav, Ruth. Pav with a tomato on top. Very Famiglia.

**Ruth:** That's disgusting, Sal.

**Sal:** That's what Tony said, Ruth. Then he ate three.

*Gurjit climbs down from his ladder, looking very pleased.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth, the sign's straight!

**Ruth:** It's not straight, Gurjit. It's still lopsided.

**Gurjit:** It's straight to me, Ruth. That's what matters.

**Ruth:** Gurjit, you're a menace.

**Gurjit:** A menace with a sign, Ruth. That's a beautiful thing.

*Sal sets the tea-towel-wrapped tin on the counter, unfolds the cloth, and taps the lid.*

**Sal:** The Don says there's a note inside.

**Ruth:** There's a note in a biscuit tin?

**Sal:** He says it's the best place for a note, Ruth. Nobody looks in the tin.

*Ruth lifts the lid. Inside is a single biscuit, crumbled to powder, and a folded piece of paper. She reads it out loud.*

**Ruth:** "Ruth. The biscuit is gone. The tin remains. Like the SALT, like the dairy, like our friendship. Vittorio."

**Sal:** That's very poetic, Ruth.

**Ruth:** That's the Don, Sal. He's a poet with a lolly.

*The bell above the door rings. Priya walks in, notebook under her arm, bow tie finally off and folded in her pocket.*

**Priya:** Hi, Ruth. Can I buy a lolly?

**Ruth:** You can buy anything, love. Except the biscuit tin.

**Priya:** I'm not buying the biscuit tin, Ruth. I'm buying a lolly for my essay.

**Ruth:** What's your essay about?

**Priya:** The SALT. The dairy. And the biscuits.

**Ruth:** That's a lot of biscuits, love.

**Priya:** It's a lot of city, Ruth. Everybody's in it.

*Ruth hands her a pineapple lolly. Priya unwraps it and looks at it thoughtfully.*

**Priya:** Ruth, what's the most important thing about this place?

**Ruth:** The lights, love. As long as the lights are on, somebody's still here.

**Priya:** That's going in the essay.

**Ruth:** Cheers, love. Mind you cite me.

## End of Part 8

**Story threads moved forward:**

- The Riccarton Boys have been dealt with by the station. Biscuits were eaten, fines were issued, and the bins will be paid for.
- Jace and Kyle have both been fired from Riccarton Motors. Kyle has been sent to recover four hubcaps by Friday, with a swimmer.
- The Night Kiwi's hat is in an evidence bag. Pip is investigating, but Hobbs thinks she's chasing ducks.
- Bill has set up a pie stall under the Night Kiwi's name. The Night Kiwi himself was seen buying a sausage roll, and paying for it.
- Velvet is donating the gold tomato painting to the Market, and the Roma is going on the pav stall.
- Vito has the empty biscuit tin on his mantelpiece, and has left a note inside it for Ruth.
- Priya has a lolly, a conclusion for her essay, and her bow tie in her pocket.

**Next:** Part 9 is the big one: Friday, the Velvet painting goes to the Market, the hubcap recovery goes wrong in the duck pond, the Night Kiwi finally shows his face, and the story starts to close.
