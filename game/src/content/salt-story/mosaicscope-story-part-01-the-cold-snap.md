# MosaicScope Story, Part 1: The Cold Snap

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Each part is a chapter of one connected story set in the SALT District and around central Christchurch.

**Format:** Scenes. Each scene has a location, time, and cast. Speakers are named in bold. `[stage directions]` are in brackets. Kiwi voice throughout. Cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Story so far:** Nothing yet. This is the opening.

---

## Scene 1: The Dairy, 6:40am

*The SALT District, a corner dairy on Tuam Street. Frost on the windscreens. Ruth is stacking milk crates. Sal is leaning on the door, pretending to read a newspaper.*

**Ruth:** Morning, bro. You're early. Or I'm late. One of us is wrong.

**Sal:** Morning, Ruth. I'm not early, I'm just not sleeping. My nonna says that's a sign.

**Ruth:** Your nonna's not a sign, Sal. She's a menace with a rolling pin.

**Sal:** She's a good woman. She's also worried about the tomatoes.

**Ruth:** Everyone's worried about the tomatoes. The whole SALT is worried about the tomatoes.

**Sal:** The Don is worried. That's different. The Don is very worried.

*Ruth stops stacking. She looks at him over the top of a crate.*

**Ruth:** Right. So what's the lollies for this week?

**Sal:** Twelve. Pineapple. Friday, like always.

**Ruth:** It's Tuesday.

**Sal:** He wants them early. He said it's a gesture of goodwill.

**Ruth:** Goodwill. Sure. You know what goodwill costs me, Sal? Two hundred bucks in stock, a broken pie warmer, and a bloke who keeps trying to buy my car.

**Sal:** That was one time.

**Ruth:** He offered me a Toyota for a dollar and a lolly. I'm not stupid, bro.

*A bell rings as the door opens. A young man in a Riccarton Motors hoodie comes in, hands in his pockets, eyes on the chip rack.*

**Jace:** Morning, Ruth. Morning, Sal. Nice day for a Famiglia meeting.

**Sal:** Jace. You're not supposed to be in here before eight.

**Jace:** Who says? The dairy's a public place, mate. Everyone's got a right to a Burger Ring.

**Ruth:** Bring one to the till, Jace, and pay for it. I'm not running a charity.

**Jace:** Yeah, nah, you're running a protection racket for a bloke who puts pineapple on his pasta.

**Sal:** That's not a crime.

**Jace:** It should be.

*Jace grabs a packet of chips, takes a long look at Sal, and slides a coin onto the counter. It's a five-cent piece.*

**Jace:** Tell the Don we're thinking about the parking.

**Sal:** Tell him yourself. He likes you when you're polite.

**Jace:** I'm always polite, Sal. I'm just loud about it.

*He leaves. The bell rings again.*

**Ruth:** Sal, what's the actual problem with the Riccarton Boys?

**Sal:** They want the SALT parking. And they keep parking in it.

**Ruth:** Then park somewhere else.

**Sal:** Ruth. That's not how the SALT works.

## Scene 2: The Bus Stop, Riccarton Road, 7:15am

*A cold southerly is blowing down the road. Priya is at the bus stop with a backpack bigger than she is. Bill is on the bench with a flask. The bus is late.*

**Priya:** Is it still coming, do you reckon?

**Bill:** Course it is. Buses are like the Cathedral. They're always coming. They're never finished.

**Priya:** That's really not comforting, Bill.

**Bill:** It wasn't meant to be. It was meant to be true.

**Priya:** I've got an assignment due Thursday, and I've not started.

**Bill:** Thursday's a long way off, love.

**Priya:** It's Wednesday tomorrow, Bill. I'm running out of Thursdays.

*A car pulls up behind them, too loud. It's a yellow hatchback with a spoiler the size of a surfboard and a bass that shakes the bench. The driver winds down the window. It's Brooke.*

**Brooke:** Oi, you lot! Have you seen Tanner?

**Bill:** Haven't seen anyone with a tracksuit this morning. Got a shock of yellow in the rain though.

**Brooke:** That's my car, Grandad.

**Bill:** I know. Shocking, isn't it?

**Priya:** Is that a Riccarton car?

**Brooke:** It's a boy racer car, love. Wrong question. The Riccarton is the crew. We're the boys.

**Priya:** Do you need a lift? The bus is late.

**Brooke:** The bus is always late. That's why I've got a car.

**Bill:** That's why you've got a car you can't afford, son.

**Brooke:** Ha. That's a good one, Bill. I'm stealing it.

*He drives off, the bass thumping across the road. Bill takes a sip from the flask.*

**Priya:** Do you think he'll get a ticket?

**Bill:** Not today. Hobbs has got his break until nine.

**Priya:** Hobbs is on his break every day.

**Bill:** That's why I like him. He's dependable.

*Long pause. The wind rattles a bin lid.*

**Priya:** Bill, can I ask you something?

**Bill:** You're going to anyway.

**Priya:** Did it ever snow here?

**Bill:** Once. In '92. Everyone thought the end of the world had come. Then the snow melted and we all went back to complaining about the wind.

**Priya:** Hannah says there should be snow in October.

**Bill:** Who's Hannah?

**Priya:** She's a snowboarder. She's been looking for snow all over town. She's furious.

**Bill:** Tell her to try the Port Hills. Best place for snow. Also best place for getting lost.

*The bus rounds the corner, lights on, groaning. Priya stands up with a sigh of relief.*

**Priya:** Oh thank god.

**Bill:** Don't thank God, love. Thank the driver. He's the one who had to put up with you all morning.

## Scene 3: Mosaic Studio, Lunchtime

*MosaicScope Studio. Warm light, old prints on the walls, a boombox on a stool. Kodak is editing photos on a laptop. Nova is making toast in a tiny kitchen. Static sits by the boombox, looking worried.*

**Nova:** Toast, anyone? It's only slightly burnt.

**Kodak:** Only slightly is my favourite amount of burnt. Hold still, Nova. Got it.

**Nova:** Don't take a photo of me with toast in my mouth.

**Kodak:** It's for the archive, bro. Art is messy.

**Static:** Is the power okay? The boombox was flickering.

**Nova:** The power's fine, Static. You're fine. Everyone's fine.

**Static:** Ok. Yeah. Ok.

**Kodak:** Static, mate, you've been like this all morning. What's going on?

**Static:** There's a storm coming. Nor'wester, they said on the radio. Hot and horrible. Power goes out in the Nor'wester.

**Nova:** It's not a storm, Static. It's just a wind. A hot, annoying wind.

**Static:** It knocked out the power at the Park last year. The whole Park. The zoo's generator went up like a chip bag.

**Kodak:** That was a fire, not a storm. Different thing.

**Static:** Same thing. Fire and wind. Wind and fire.

**Nova:** Okay. Let's make a plan. If the power goes out, we'll light a candle and play music on the phone.

**Static:** Phone's got no battery. Lux borrowed the charger for a bottle cap.

**Kodak:** Lux has a lot of charger-based hobbies.

*The door bangs open. Lux comes in, out of breath, holding something up like a trophy.*

**Lux:** Look! Look what I found!

**Kodak:** What is it?

**Lux:** It's a bottle cap! A gold one! Look how shiny!

**Nova:** It's lovely, Lux. Where'd you find it?

**Lux:** In the bin behind the dairy. Ruth said I wasn't allowed in the bin. I'm not in the bin, I'm next to the bin.

**Kodak:** That's a very fine line, Lux.

**Lux:** Bro, I'm an expert in fine lines. I've been looking at the Cathedral for hours.

*Static suddenly sits up straight, staring at the window.*

**Static:** Did you guys see the sky?

**Nova:** What about it?

**Static:** It's green.

**Kodak:** It's not green. It's a bit yellowy.

**Static:** Green-yellow. That's a storm colour. Bad storm colour.

**Lux:** It's a nice colour, though. Like a lolly.

**Nova:** Okay, everyone. Let's not panic. We'll check the radio. Static, you hold the candle. Kodak, grab the torch. Lux, stop eating the lolly. Sorry, that's not a lolly, that's a bottle cap.

**Lux:** It's a treasure.

## Scene 4: The Dairy, Afternoon

*The dairy is busier now. Customers come and go. Ruth is trying to read a letter from the council while Gurjit leans on the counter with a pizza box.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth, you're not going to believe this.

**Ruth:** Try me, bro. I've had a morning like a bag of spanners.

**Gurjit:** The council has sent me a letter. About my pizza sign.

**Ruth:** What's wrong with your pizza sign?

**Gurjit:** It's too bright. They say it's disrupting the Cathedral view.

**Ruth:** The Cathedral view is scaffolding, bro. Nobody can see it.

**Gurjit:** That's what I said! But they want the sign down by Friday. And I've got a whole stack of pineapple pizzas.

**Ruth:** Then move the pineapple.

**Gurjit:** Move the pineapple? Ruth, it's the topping. It's my identity.

*A customer comes in holding a bag of lollies. It's Vito's man, Sal, who slides in silently behind him.*

**Sal:** Afternoon, Ruth. Afternoon, Gurjit. Nice pizza.

**Gurjit:** Sal, you're not allowed in the dairy before Friday.

**Sal:** It's Tuesday, I know. The Don's got a gesture of goodwill. Want to hear it?

**Ruth:** No, Sal. I don't want to hear it.

**Sal:** He wants to talk. About the tomatoes.

**Ruth:** Sal, I'm not a tomato expert.

**Sal:** No, but you've got a lot of lollies, and the Don loves a lolly.

**Gurjit:** Just tell him to come in, Sal. He can have a lolly and a chat.

**Sal:** He doesn't come in, Gurjit. He sends people. Like me. Like the Riccarton Boys.

*Silence. The fridge hums.*

**Ruth:** Sal, did the Riccarton Boys say something to you?

**Sal:** They said the dairy's in trouble if the Don doesn't pay for the parking.

**Ruth:** The dairy's always in trouble, Sal. It's a dairy.

**Gurjit:** I've got a pizza that says otherwise.

*Ruth takes a deep breath and puts her hand on the counter.*

**Ruth:** Okay. Here's the deal. I'll talk to Vito. But I want a cheaper protection rate, and I want the Riccarton Boys off my pavement.

**Sal:** Ruth, that's not how it works. The Don doesn't do deals.

**Ruth:** Then he doesn't get the lollies.

**Sal:** He gets the lollies, Ruth. He always gets the lollies.

*Sal slides a coin onto the counter, looks at it for a long moment, and walks out. The bell rings.*

**Gurjit:** That went well.

**Ruth:** No, it didn't. But he didn't pay for the chips either.

## Scene 5: Riccarton Road, Evening

*The lights are changing. Jace and Tanner are standing next to a black ute with a missing hubcap. The street lights hum. A Riccarton Motors sign flickers in the distance.*

**Tanner:** Mate, the hubs are sick. Look at them. All four.

**Jace:** Three, bro. One's in the boot.

**Tanner:** Okay, three hubs and a boot hub. Still sick.

**Jace:** We need the parking. The SALT parking. Famiglia's been sitting on it for years.

**Tanner:** Famiglia are just a bunch of blokes who eat pasta.

**Jace:** They're blokes with lollies. And lollies are power, bro.

**Tanner:** Lollies are sticky, bro.

*Brooke's yellow hatchback rolls up, loud, the bass shaking the mirrors.*

**Brooke:** Oi! Did you hear? The Mosaics are throwing a party tonight.

**Jace:** At the stage?

**Brooke:** At the stage. Tiles reckons it's going to be sick. Ruby's doing the foam fight.

**Tanner:** Ruby's going to smash someone.

**Jace:** That's the point, bro. Let's go. We'll crash it.

**Tanner:** Crash it? We can't crash a party, bro. It's Tiles.

**Jace:** Yeah, and Tiles has got the lollies in the chilly bin. Think about it. Free lollies and free parking.

**Brooke:** You're such a Famiglia.

**Jace:** I'm not a Famiglia, I'm a Riccarton man. I just know where the good food is.

**Tanner:** Honest, I just want to see Flash get smacked with a foam sword.

*A police car rolls slowly past, lights off. It stops. Constable Pip leans out.*

**Pip:** Evening, boys. Looking sharp.

**Jace:** Evening, officer. Lovely night for a drive.

**Pip:** Lovely night for a burnout, more like. Anything I should know about?

**Jace:** Nah, nothing, officer. Just checking the hubs.

**Pip:** Good. Keep them checked. And keep them on the car, yeah?

**Tanner:** Yeah, officer.

*Pip drives off slowly. Once she's gone, Brooke lets out a whoop.*

**Brooke:** She's a legend, that one. Doesn't even look at the hubs.

**Jace:** She looks at everything. That's the scary part.

**Tanner:** Okay, party tonight. But I'm not wearing the tracksuit.

**Jace:** You're wearing the tracksuit, Tanner. It's a uniform.

## Scene 6: The SALT District, Late Night

*A street corner. A streetlight is flickering. A shopfront has a sign that says "Velvet Lounge, Members Only." The wind is picking up, rattling bins. Night Kiwi sits on a rooftop above, silhouetted against the sky. Below, Vito stands with Sal, holding a tiny bag of pineapple lollies.*

**Vito:** Sal. Listen to the wind.

**Sal:** It's a Nor'wester, Don. It's hot and it smells like bins.

**Vito:** It smells like trouble. Those Riccarton boys are at the stage tonight.

**Sal:** Tiles' party.

**Vito:** Tiles throws parties the way a dog throws up. Everywhere, and at the worst time.

**Sal:** Should we go?

**Vito:** We don't go to parties, Sal. We send a gift.

**Sal:** What gift?

**Vito:** A crate of lollies. One for every crew member. And a note that says, "Enjoy, the Don is watching."

**Sal:** That's a bit creepy, Don.

**Vito:** It's a bit creepy. That's the point.

*A bin clatters loudly. Both men jump. A shadow moves on the rooftop above.*

**Sal:** Don, did you see that?

**Vito:** I always see that. It's the Night Kiwi. He's been up there for three nights, watching my tomatoes.

**Sal:** Why would he watch tomatoes?

**Vito:** Because he's a fool, Sal. Or a genius. I've not decided. Either way, he's got a very nice hat.

**Sal:** Don, I think the tomatoes are dying.

**Vito:** Sal. The tomatoes are not dying. They're being very dramatic. Like Tony at the casino.

*From above, a voice rolls down, dry and dramatic.*

**Night Kiwi:** You're both going to regret the lollies, Vito.

**Vito:** Ah, Kiwi! Come down, my friend. I'll buy you a pineapple lolly.

**Night Kiwi:** I don't do lollies. I do justice.

**Vito:** Justice tastes like pineapple, Kiwi. Ask anyone.

*The Night Kiwi drops from the roof, landing softly on the bins. He's wearing a dark jacket and a mask shaped like a kiwi bird. He holds up one hand.*

**Night Kiwi:** Tonight, the Riccarton Boys are going to steal your parking, Vito. And you're going to do nothing about it.

**Vito:** Do nothing? I am the nothing, my friend. I am the master of nothing.

**Night Kiwi:** Then watch the dairy. That's where it starts.

*He vanishes back up the drainpipe. Vito stares after him.*

**Vito:** Sal. Call Ruth. Tell her to watch the dairy.

**Sal:** Don, Ruth's the one who told us to get lost.

**Vito:** Then tell her I'm sorry.

**Sal:** You're sorry?

**Vito:** For the lollies. Tell her they're on me. Every one.

*Sal looks at the bag. Slowly, he tips out a single lolly and holds it up.*

**Sal:** That's one lolly, Don.

**Vito:** Then it's a very expensive apology.

## End of Part 1

**Story threads opened:**

- Ruth is being squeezed by the Famiglia over protection money, and has made a counter-offer.
- The Riccarton Boys want the SALT parking and are planning to crash Tiles' party.
- The Night Kiwi has warned about the dairy.
- Static has a worry about a Nor'wester and a green sky, which could mean a blackout.
- Gurjit has a council problem with his pizza sign.
- Priya has an assignment due and a bus that's late.
- Bill knows everything about the city's history, and is probably the only one who remembers the snow in '92.
