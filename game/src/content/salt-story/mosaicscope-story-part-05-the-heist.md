# MosaicScope Story, Part 5: The Heist

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1: "The Cold Snap." Part 2: "The Nor'wester." Part 3: "The Morning After." Part 4: "The Meeting."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 4:** The Velvet gala is in full swing. Vito has arrived with Sal and an empty box to take back his tomato painting. The Mosaics have crashed in. The Night Kiwi is on the gallery, watching. Pip and Kyle are chasing the decoy ute across town.

---

## Scene 1: The Gala Floor, Moments Later

*The lights stay dimmed. The string quartet has stopped mid-note. Guests drift back from the centre of the room, murmuring. Velvet stands at the top of the staircase, one hand on the banister, the other still holding his champagne.*

**Velvet:** Everyone, please. Remain calm. This is a gala, not a riot.

**Flash:** It's a bit of a riot, bro.

**Velvet:** Flash, darling. You're not invited.

**Flash:** I'm invited to the painting, Ponsonby. The painting's been waiting for me.

**Velvet:** The painting has not been waiting for you, Flash.

**Flash:** It's been glaring at me since six. Mikey said so.

*Mikey Stone, at the front door, shrugs and holds up his hands.*

**Mikey:** I said it was glaring. I didn't say it wanted to meet you.

**Honey:** Okay, team. Here's the plan. Tiles, you distract Velvet. Ruby, you guard the door. Flash, you stay away from the chandelier. Glitter, you keep the champagne away from everyone's shoes. Priya, you take notes. Vito, Sal, you do whatever you were going to do anyway.

**Tiles:** Honey, that's not a plan. That's a list.

**Honey:** It's a colour-coded list, Tiles. It counts.

**Vito:** Honey, my dear. You are a very organised girl. I approve.

**Honey:** Thank you, Vito. I've been waiting for someone to notice.

**Vito:** I notice everything, my dear. I have eyes in the tomatoes.

*Sal leans in to Vito, whispering, holding the empty wooden box.*

**Sal:** Don, the painting's on the wall, behind the staircase. It's got a security guard.

**Vito:** Ah. A guard. Ponsonby always hires the big ones.

**Sal:** He's the size of a fridge, Don.

**Vito:** Then we'll take the painting from the fridge's pocket.

**Sal:** That doesn't make sense, Don.

**Vito:** It doesn't have to make sense, Sal. It has to be tasty.

## Scene 2: The Painting Wall

*Behind the staircase, a corridor lit by a single gold lamp. The gold tomato painting hangs in a heavy frame. A security guard the size of a small wardrobe stands next to it, arms crossed. His name tag reads "BIG DEAN." Mo the busker is leaning against the wall beside him, guitar case open, playing a very quiet tune.*

**Mo:** Evening, mate. Want a song? I've got one about a tomato.

**Big Dean:** No.

**Mo:** It's a short one. Four verses.

**Big Dean:** I said no.

**Mo:** Fair enough. It's a very sad tomato, mate. It's been through a lot.

**Big Dean:** Why are you here, busker?

**Mo:** Tips, mate. Velvet doesn't tip. But I do like a challenge.

*Priya comes around the corner, notebook under one arm, bow tie slightly crooked. She stops when she sees Big Dean.*

**Priya:** Hi! I'm writing an essay about the painting.

**Big Dean:** Don't touch it.

**Priya:** I'm not going to touch it! I just want a photo.

**Big Dean:** No photos.

**Priya:** It's for my uni assignment. About urban identity.

**Big Dean:** Urban identity's not a painting, love.

**Priya:** It's a tomato, though. In gold. It's kind of identity.

**Big Dean:** It's Velvet's identity. He's very particular about gold.

*Kodak appears from behind a column, camera at his eye. The flash goes off. Big Dean blinks.*

**Kodak:** Smile, mate. Got it.

**Big Dean:** Did you just take my photo?

**Kodak:** I took the tomato's photo, bro. You were just in the background.

**Big Dean:** I'm not in the background. I'm the security.

**Kodak:** You're very photogenic, mate. Like a bodyguard in a film.

**Big Dean:** Thanks, I think.

*Lux slides across the floor on her socks, holding a silver bottle cap high above her head.*

**Lux:** Hi! I'm a treasure hunter! I found a gold one!

**Big Dean:** Kid, you can't be here.

**Lux:** I'm not a kid, I'm a treasure hunter! There's a difference!

**Big Dean:** Go back to the party, kid.

**Lux:** The party's over there! I'm in the treasure room!

**Big Dean:** There's no treasure room.

**Lux:** There's a painting of a tomato. That's the treasure room, bro.

*Big Dean looks at her, then at the painting, then back at her. He shakes his head.*

**Big Dean:** That's the most sensible thing anyone's said all night.

**Lux:** Thanks, bro. I'm very sensible.

*A crash from the other end of the corridor. Everyone turns. Tiles stands at the end, wearing a shiny gold jacket, holding a champagne bottle like a trophy.*

**Tiles:** Oi, Big Dean! Come and have a drink, bro!

**Big Dean:** I'm on duty, mate.

**Tiles:** Duty's boring. Come and have a lolly.

**Big Dean:** I don't like lollies.

**Tiles:** Then have a foam sword. Flash! Get the foam sword!

*Flash bursts from the shadows at the other end, brandishing a foam sword. He swings it wildly at the lamp.*

**Flash:** Who wants it?

**Big Dean:** Not you, mate.

**Flash:** Nobody wants it, Dean. That's the trouble with this town.

*Big Dean takes a step towards Flash, cracking his knuckles. The corridor lights flicker. Mo suddenly starts strumming very loudly.*

**Mo:** Sing along, everyone! It's a song about a tomato!

**Big Dean:** Shut up, busker!

**Mo:** No, mate. It's a very good song.

*In the chaos, Sal slips past, carrying the empty wooden box. He reaches up, lifts the gold tomato painting off its hook, and holds it against his chest.*

**Sal:** Don? I've got it.

**Vito (off-screen):** Sal! Be careful! The gold leaf chips!

**Sal:** It's a painting, Don. It doesn't chip, it just gets dramatic.

## Scene 3: The Staircase

*The painting is in Sal's arms. He's moving carefully down the staircase, eyes on the frame. Velvet is at the bottom of the stairs, waiting, arms folded.*

**Velvet:** Sal. Darling. Where are you going with my tomato?

**Sal:** It's not your tomato, Sir Ponsonby. It's the Don's.

**Velvet:** It's my painting, Sal. I bought it. It's a tribute.

**Sal:** It's a tribute to the Don's tomato, Sir. And the Don wants it back.

**Velvet:** The Don can have it when I'm finished admiring it.

**Sal:** The Don's finished admiring it, Sir. He's been admiring it since six.

*Vito appears at the top of the staircase, lolly still in hand, looking regal.*

**Vito:** Ponsonby. Let the boy pass.

**Velvet:** Vittorio! You're making a scene.

**Vito:** I'm not making a scene, Ponsonby. I'm making a tomato. And that tomato belongs in my garden.

**Velvet:** It belongs on my wall, Vittorio. It's a masterpiece.

**Vito:** It's a painting of a tomato, Ponsonby. A tomato that cannot be eaten.

**Velvet:** That's the beauty of it, darling. It can't be eaten. It can only be admired.

**Vito:** Then admire it from a distance, Ponsonby. Sal, bring the painting down.

*Sal descends the last step, painting clutched to his chest, eyes wide. Velvet steps forward, blocking his path. Sal stops. The two men stare at each other.*

**Velvet:** You're not taking my tomato, Sal.

**Sal:** I'm not taking it, Sir Ponsonby. I'm carrying it.

**Velvet:** There's a difference?

**Sal:** There's always a difference, Sir. Ask my nonna.

*Ruth steps out from behind a potted palm, holding a dairy tin in one hand.*

**Ruth:** Morning, Ponsonby. Evening, I mean. Sorry, it's been a long day.

**Velvet:** Ruth! Darling! You're here!

**Ruth:** I'm here for the biscuits, Ponsonby. And the painting.

**Velvet:** You're not invited, Ruth.

**Ruth:** Priya's got a bow tie, Ponsonby. I'm wearing the other half of the outfit.

**Velvet:** That's not an outfit, darling.

**Ruth:** It's a dairy uniform, Ponsonby. It's the most respectable thing in this room.

*Ruth opens the dairy tin. Inside are a dozen stale biscuits and, on top, a single folded note.*

**Ruth:** Vito. Ponsonby. You both need a biscuit. Sit down.

**Vito:** Ruth, this is not the time for biscuits.

**Ruth:** It's always the time for biscuits, Vito. That's how you know it's a good day.

## Scene 4: The Gallery, Above

*The gallery is dark. A narrow balcony overlooks the gala floor. The Night Kiwi crouches on the railing, binoculars in one hand. Pip climbs up the back stairs, gasping, Kyle trailing behind holding a torch and a rag.*

**Pip:** Night Kiwi! I've got you!

**Night Kiwi:** You've got a chance, officer. That's not the same thing.

**Pip:** Why are you up here?

**Night Kiwi:** To watch the heist.

**Pip:** It's not a heist, bro. It's a gala.

**Night Kiwi:** It's a heist with lollies, officer. Look down there.

*Pip leans over the railing. Below, the gala floor is a whirl of gold, champagne, and foam swords. Vito and Velvet stand face to face, arguing. Sal stands between them with the painting.*

**Pip:** That's Vito and Velvet. Why's Sal got a painting?

**Night Kiwi:** Because Sal is the only sensible one in the room, officer.

**Kyle (from the stairs, out of breath):** Pip, the decoy's gone.

**Pip:** Gone where, Kyle?

**Kyle:** Gone into the Park, officer. It drove into the duck pond.

**Pip:** The duck pond? Why?

**Kyle:** It's got a lot of bass, officer. Ducks don't like bass.

**Night Kiwi:** That's the most honest thing I've heard all night.

*Kyle stares at the Night Kiwi.*

**Kyle:** Who are you?

**Night Kiwi:** A rumour, Kyle. Keep moving.

**Kyle:** Are you the bloke with the kiwi hat?

**Night Kiwi:** It's a statement, Kyle. Please don't ask.

**Pip:** Night Kiwi, what's going to happen to the painting?

**Night Kiwi:** That depends on who's holding it when the lights come back on.

*Below, the chandelier flickers. The room goes black. Everyone shouts at once. A foam sword clatters across the floor.*

**Night Kiwi:** Now, officer. Now it starts.

## Scene 5: The Dark Gala Floor

*Pitch black, then a sudden flash of a camera. Kodak's flash. Then another. Then Glitter's phone torch, sweeping across the crowd. People are shouting, champagne glasses smashing, a single foam sword whistling through the air.*

**Glitter (off-screen):** Okay! Nobody panic! I've got my good side on!

**Velvet (off-screen):** Who turned off the lights? Darling, this is unacceptable!

**Flash (off-screen):** It's a blackout, Ponsonby! It's the Nor'wester's fault!

**Velvet (off-screen):** The Nor'wester's been outside all day!

**Flash (off-screen):** Then it's come inside, bro!

*A thud. Somebody screams. Then laughter.*

**Honey (off-screen):** Everybody stay where you are. I'm counting.

**Tiles (off-screen):** What are you counting, Honey?

**Honey (off-screen):** Heads. And lollies. Mostly lollies.

*Glitter's phone light swings across the stairs. It lands on Sal, who is halfway down with the painting, Vito at his elbow. Ruth is right behind them, the dairy tin in her arms.*

**Glitter:** Sal! Ruth! Where's the painting going?

**Sal:** Out the back, Glitter. Vito says the back door's got a lovely view of the car park.

**Glitter:** Is Vito taking it?

**Sal:** Vito's borrowing it, Glitter.

**Glitter:** Borrowing's a bit rude, Sal.

**Sal:** It's borrowing with feelings, Glitter.

*A figure drops silently from the balcony and lands on the staircase, between them. It's the Night Kiwi, crouched low, a kiwi mask on his face.*

**Night Kiwi:** Going somewhere, Vito?

**Vito:** Kiwi! My friend! You've come to see the painting!

**Night Kiwi:** I've come to see who's holding it.

**Vito:** Sal is holding it.

**Night Kiwi:** Sal is holding it for you, Vito. That's the problem.

**Vito:** Is it a problem, Kiwi? I thought it was a tribute.

**Night Kiwi:** A tribute with a getaway car, Vito. That's a problem.

*Vito pauses. He looks at Sal. Then he looks at the painting. Then he looks at Ruth.*

**Vito:** Ruth. What do you think?

**Ruth:** I think it's a painting of a tomato, Vito. It's not worth the trouble.

**Vito:** But it's my tomato, Ruth.

**Ruth:** It's your tomato, Vito. But it's not a tomato. It's gold paint. You can't water it. You can't eat it. You can't even give it to your nonna.

**Vito:** Madonna. You're right.

*Vito turns to Sal.*

**Vito:** Sal. Put the painting down.

**Sal:** Don?

**Vito:** Put it down. Gently. It's a painting of a tomato. We're not thieves.

**Sal:** We were thieves five minutes ago, Don.

**Vito:** Five minutes is a long time for a thief, Sal. Put it down.

*Sal carefully lowers the painting to the stairs and leans it against the wall. He sighs with relief.*

**Sal:** Thank you, Don.

**Vito:** Don't thank me, Sal. Thank the Night Kiwi. He's the one who ruined my fun.

**Night Kiwi:** You ruined your own fun, Vito. I just turned the lights on.

*The lights flicker back on. The room blinks, startled. Everyone squints at the staircase.*

## Scene 6: The Painting, After

*The gala floor is a mess: smashed glasses, scattered gold paper, a single foam sword stuck in a chandelier, and a very wet Flash sitting on the floor. Velvet stands at the top of the stairs, looking deflated.*

**Velvet:** Well. That's the most exciting gala I've ever hosted.

**Mikey:** Sir, the guests are asking for their money back.

**Velvet:** Tell them the money's in the painting, Mikey.

**Mikey:** It's a painting of a tomato, sir.

**Velvet:** It's a very expensive tomato, Mikey.

*Priya walks up the stairs, notebook open, bow tie even more crooked.*

**Priya:** Sir Ponsonby, can I ask you a question for my essay?

**Velvet:** Darling, you can ask me anything. Except about the price.

**Priya:** Why did you buy the painting, if you don't even like tomatoes?

**Velvet:** Because it's the only thing in this city that everyone's willing to fight over, darling. Even if it's just a tomato.

**Priya:** That's really deep, sir.

**Velvet:** I'm a very deep man, darling. Also very rich. Don't forget the rich bit.

*Vito, Sal and Ruth come up the stairs, the painting between them. Vito holds a lolly out to Velvet.*

**Vito:** Ponsonby. A lolly. For your trouble.

**Velvet:** A pineapple lolly, Vittorio?

**Vito:** The only kind I respect.

**Velvet:** Thank you. I'll keep it on the wall, next to the painting.

**Vito:** Don't put it on the wall, Ponsonby. Eat it.

*Velvet laughs and unwraps the lolly. He pops it into his mouth. Everyone waits. He chews slowly.*

**Velvet:** Hm. It's not bad.

**Vito:** It's a lolly, Ponsonby. It's not a painting.

**Velvet:** That's why it's better, Vittorio.

*Tiles walks up behind them, shiny gold jacket dripping champagne, Honey at his side with her clipboard.*

**Tiles:** Oi! Party's moved to the Market! Everyone's invited!

**Honey:** It's on the list, Tiles.

**Tiles:** Honey, everything's on the list.

**Honey:** That's the problem, Tiles. The list is getting too long.

**Ruby (off-screen):** Honey, you say that every week!

**Honey:** And every week, it's true, Ruby!

*Ruth turns to Vito. She holds out the dairy tin.*

**Ruth:** Vito. A biscuit?

**Vito:** A biscuit, Ruth? After all this?

**Ruth:** It's stale, Vito. But it's the thought that counts.

*Vito takes one. He bites. Long, long pause.*

**Vito:** Ruth. This is the worst biscuit I've ever eaten.

**Ruth:** I know, bro. That's why I brought it.

*Vito starts laughing. Velvet starts laughing. Then Sal. Then everyone.*

---

## Scene 7: Duck Pond, Dawn

*The Park's duck pond at first light. Mist rises off the water. The decoy ute sits halfway submerged, one wheel up, bass still faintly thumping. Pip stands on the bank in a wet uniform. Kyle sits on a bench, rag over his face. Hobbs arrives in the patrol car, carrying a coffee in each hand.*

**Hobbs:** Morning, Pip. Why does my car smell like a duck?

**Pip:** It's the decoy, Sarge. It went for a swim.

**Hobbs:** The decoy? Where's the hatchback?

**Pip:** Long gone, Sarge. Jace got away again.

**Hobbs:** Brilliant. And the tomato?

**Pip:** That's the thing, Sarge. The tomato's at the gala.

**Hobbs:** The gold one?

**Pip:** Yeah. The gold one. Vito got it back, then gave it to Velvet, then Velvet ate a lolly.

**Hobbs:** Pip, you're making less sense than usual.

**Pip:** It's been a long night, Sarge.

*Kyle lifts the rag from his face.*

**Kyle:** Sarge, can I have the coffee?

**Hobbs:** Is that a Kiwi hat on the bench?

**Kyle:** Nah, Sarge. It's just a hat.

**Hobbs:** Right. Put it in the report, Pip. "Duck pond, decoy, Night Kiwi's hat. Probably not important."

**Pip:** Under what heading, Sarge?

**Hobbs:** "Riccarton Boys, still at large, and the ducks are very upset."

## End of Part 5

**Story threads moved forward:**

- The gold Roma painting was returned to the Velvet wall, but Velvet has eaten a pineapple lolly from Vito, and the two men have an uneasy truce.
- Ruth has a new alliance with Vito, built on a stale biscuit.
- Jace has escaped again in the real hatchback. The decoy ute is in the duck pond.
- Priya has her essay conclusion: that the city fights over things that aren't really worth fighting over.
- The Night Kiwi's identity is still a mystery to Pip and Kyle. Hobbs has noticed the hat.
- Tiles has moved the party to the Market.
