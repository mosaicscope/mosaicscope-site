# MosaicScope Story, Part 6: The Revenge

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1: "The Cold Snap." Part 2: "The Nor'wester." Part 3: "The Morning After." Part 4: "The Meeting." Part 5: "The Heist."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 5:** The gold Roma painting is back on Velvet's wall, and Velvet has eaten one of Vito's lollies. Ruth and Vito have a new, uneasy truce. Jace escaped again. The decoy ute is in the duck pond. Hobbs has noticed the Night Kiwi's hat and decided not to write it down.

---

## Scene 1: Riccarton Road, Morning After

*Early sun over the dealerships. Traffic cones are scattered across the road. The Riccarton Motors sign is broken, flickering "RICCARTON MOTO" in red. Kyle is hosing down the forecourt. Jace stands by the showroom window, cap low, arms folded, looking at a row of cars that now have their hubcaps gone.*

**Kyle:** Jace, you're going to have to explain the hubcaps.

**Jace:** I'm not explaining anything, Kyle. The hubcaps explain themselves.

**Kyle:** They're gone, mate. All four. Off the showroom cars.

**Jace:** Yeah, well, the Famiglia are probably wearing them as earrings.

**Kyle:** The Famiglia don't wear hubcaps, Jace. Vito wears pinstripes and he's a bit precious.

**Jace:** Then who did it?

**Kyle:** You, bro. You did it. You told Tanner and Brooke to take them as a "fundraiser."

**Jace:** It was a fundraiser, Kyle. For the Riccarton Boys.

**Kyle:** It was a theft, Jace. In front of the boss.

*Tanner walks up from the car park, holding two hubcaps, one under each arm. He looks very pleased with himself.*

**Tanner:** Morning, bros! Look what I found in the bin behind the dairy!

**Jace:** Those are ours, Tanner.

**Tanner:** They were ours, Jace. Now they're in the bin, which makes them everyone's. That's a communal hubcap.

**Kyle:** Tanner, put them back on the cars before the boss sees.

**Tanner:** The boss is on his way, Kyle. He's got a cup of coffee and a face like thunder.

*A dark sedan pulls up outside. Out steps a man in a crisp grey suit, sunglasses pushed up on his head. This is Mr Dwyer, the dealership owner. He surveys the cones, the hubcaps, the flickering sign.*

**Mr Dwyer:** Jace. Explain.

**Jace:** It's a community project, Mr Dwyer.

**Mr Dwyer:** It's a crime scene, Jace. My hubcaps are in a bin. My sign says "RICCARTON MOTO." And there are cones on the Cathedral side of the road.

**Jace:** The cones were there when we got here, Mr Dwyer.

**Mr Dwyer:** I'm not blaming you for the cones, Jace. I'm blaming you for the hubcaps.

**Jace:** That's fair, Mr Dwyer.

**Mr Dwyer:** And the burnout marks on the forecourt. Those are yours too.

**Jace:** Those ones were the Famiglia, Mr Dwyer. They tried to show us up.

**Mr Dwyer:** The Famiglia don't do burnouts, Jace. They drive like they're late for a funeral.

**Jace:** Exactly, Mr Dwyer. That's why we did it. To make them look bad.

*Mr Dwyer sighs and takes off his sunglasses. He looks very tired.*

**Mr Dwyer:** Jace. You're a good kid. But you're going to get the whole crew arrested.

**Jace:** Nah, Mr Dwyer. The cops are on their break.

**Mr Dwyer:** The cops are never on their break, Jace. They've got a tomato to guard.

## Scene 2: The Dairy, Late Morning

*The dairy is quiet. Sunlight slants across the counter. Ruth is sitting on a milk crate with a cup of tea, the dairy tin of biscuits open on her knee. Gurjit is at the counter, holding a sign that reads "PIZZA SIGN STAYS." Sal is at the door, carrying a small paper bag.*

**Ruth:** Sal, you're early again.

**Sal:** I'm not early, Ruth. I'm on time for the first time in my life.

**Ruth:** Congratulations. What's in the bag?

**Sal:** Lollies. From the Don.

**Ruth:** Sal, I don't want lollies. I've had enough lollies this week to fill a bath.

**Sal:** It's not lollies, Ruth. It's a thank you.

**Ruth:** For what?

**Sal:** For the biscuit. The Don says it was the worst biscuit he's ever eaten, and he's never felt so alive.

**Ruth:** That's a weird thing to thank someone for, Sal.

**Sal:** It's a very Famiglia thing, Ruth. We don't do thank yous. We do biscuits.

*Ruth takes the bag and looks inside. Gold foil, pineapple lollies, and a small handwritten note: "Ruth, the best biscuit tin in the SALT. V."*

**Ruth:** Hm. He's a bit soft, your Don.

**Sal:** He's a lot soft, Ruth. He cried at the gala.

**Ruth:** Sal, I'm not surprised. He cries at pizza adverts.

**Gurjit:** He cried at my pizza, Ruth. He said it was a masterpiece.

**Ruth:** Gurjit, the pizza is always a masterpiece. It's pineapple.

**Gurjit:** Thank you, Ruth!

**Sal:** Anyway. The Don wants to talk. About the Riccarton Boys.

**Ruth:** Oh no. Not the Riccarton Boys.

**Sal:** They've been stealing hubcaps from the dealership. And they've been stealing the Famiglia's parking.

**Ruth:** So what does the Don want me to do about it?

**Sal:** He wants you to watch the dairy. He says the Riccarton Boys will come here next.

**Ruth:** Sal, why would they come here? I sell milk. I've got no parking.

**Sal:** You've got a dairy, Ruth. The Riccarton Boys want the dairy.

**Ruth:** For what?

**Sal:** For the biscuits, Ruth. Everyone wants the biscuits.

*The bell above the door rings. A young man in a bright tracksuit walks in, sunglasses on, holding a single hubcap. It's Brooke.*

**Brooke:** Morning, Ruth! Morning, Sal! Morning, pizza!

**Gurjit:** It's a sign, Brooke!

**Brooke:** It's a pizza-shaped sign, bro. I can tell.

**Ruth:** Brooke, what are you doing with a hubcap in my shop?

**Brooke:** Trading, Ruth. It's a hubcap for a biscuit.

**Ruth:** There's no trading in the dairy, Brooke. Only milk and chips.

**Brooke:** Then milk and chips it is. I'll take two chips and a hubcap for the drinks fridge.

**Ruth:** The drinks fridge doesn't take hubcaps, Brooke.

**Brooke:** Everything takes hubcaps, Ruth. You just need to ask nicely.

*Ruth stands up slowly. She walks to the counter, takes the hubcap from Brooke, and looks at it closely.*

**Ruth:** This isn't a hubcap, Brooke. It's a lid. From the Burger Joint bin.

**Brooke:** Same thing, Ruth.

**Ruth:** It's not the same thing, bro. Hubcaps go on cars. Lids go on bins.

**Brooke:** Then I'll put it on a bin, Ruth.

**Ruth:** Brooke. Get out.

**Brooke:** Oh, come on, Ruth! It's a joke!

**Ruth:** It's a joke that costs me a bin lid, Brooke. Out.

*Brooke shrugs, grins, and leaves. The bell rings. Sal and Gurjit stare at Ruth.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth, you're amazing.

**Sal:** Ruth, the Don's going to love you.

**Ruth:** I don't want the Don to love me, Sal. I want the Riccarton Boys to find a different bin.

## Scene 3: Riccarton Road, Afternoon

*A line of cars with their bonnets up. A handful of Riccarton Boys are standing around the middle of the road, in a loose circle, passing a can of something fizzy. Tanner is in the centre, shouting. Jace is sitting on a traffic cone, looking bored.*

**Tanner:** Okay, lads! It's the revenge tour! We're going to the Famiglia's dairy tonight!

**Brooke:** Tanner, that's Ruth's dairy.

**Tanner:** It's the Famiglia's dairy, Brooke. Ruth's just working there.

**Jace:** It's Ruth's dairy, Tanner. She owns it.

**Tanner:** She owns the stock, Jace. The Famiglia own the lollies. That's the difference.

**Jace:** Tanner, mate, you're not making sense.

**Tanner:** I make sense to the Riccarton Boys, Jace. That's all that matters.

*Pip's patrol car rolls slowly into view, lights off, window down. Pip leans out, sunglasses on, eating a sausage roll.*

**Pip:** Afternoon, boys. What's the revenge tour?

**Tanner:** Nothing, officer. Just a parade.

**Pip:** A parade of hubcaps?

**Brooke:** Officer, we don't have any hubcaps.

**Pip:** Brooke, you're holding one.

**Brooke:** This is a bin lid, officer.

**Pip:** It's shiny, Brooke. That's a hubcap.

*Pip gets out of the car, walks over, and holds out her hand. Brooke hands over the lid. Pip looks at it, then at the boys.*

**Pip:** This is from the Burger Joint bin.

**Brooke:** You're very observant, officer.

**Pip:** I'm very bored, Brooke. There's a difference.

**Jace:** Officer, it's a bit hot for a sausage roll, isn't it?

**Pip:** It's a warm sausage roll, Jace. That's the point.

*A car horn honks from the far end of the road. Everyone turns. Kyle's ute trundles around the corner, hazard lights on, bonnet tied down with a bungee cord.*

**Kyle:** Oi! Pip! Jace! Get out of the road!

**Pip:** Kyle, what's wrong now?

**Kyle:** The hatchback's been stolen! From the back lot!

**Jace:** Kyle, mate, I'm sitting right here.

**Kyle:** Not your hatchback, Jace. The Velvet hatchback.

**Pip:** The Velvet hatchback?

**Kyle:** Yeah, the one with the gold tomato sticker. Somebody's nicked it from the gala.

**Pip:** Who would steal a hatchback with a tomato sticker?

**Kyle:** Vito, officer.

*Everyone is silent. Pip raises an eyebrow.*

**Pip:** Vito's got a getaway car?

**Kyle:** Sal's driving it, officer. He says it's a loan.

**Pip:** A loan from Velvet?

**Kyle:** No, officer. A loan from the Riccarton Boys. Jace lent it to Sal.

**Jace:** Kyle, mate, I didn't lend anything to Sal!

**Kyle:** You did, bro. In exchange for the bin lids.

**Pip:** Jace, is this true?

**Jace:** Officer, that's a very complicated trade.

**Pip:** Jace, I'm writing this down.

*Pip pulls out her notebook. Jace slides off the traffic cone, grabs his cap, and sprints down the road. Tanner and Brooke scatter in different directions.*

**Pip:** Oh, you're kidding me.

**Kyle:** Pip, do you need a lift?

**Pip:** No, Kyle. I need a shower.

## Scene 4: Static's Studio, Night

*The MosaicScope Studio is lit only by a single lamp and the red glow of the boombox. Static sits in a corner, headphones on, eyes closed, rocking gently. Nova sits beside him, holding a flask of tea. Kodak is at the desk, scrolling through photos on his laptop. Lux is curled up on a beanbag, quietly sorting bottle caps by colour.*

**Nova:** Here, Static. Tea. It's got honey in it.

**Static:** Honey's bad for the decks, Nova.

**Nova:** It's not for the decks, Static. It's for you.

**Static:** Oh. Thanks. Ok.

*Static takes the flask and sips slowly. He opens his eyes.*

**Static:** Ok. Yeah. Ok.

**Kodak:** You've been quiet all night, mate.

**Static:** The power's going to go, Kodak. I can feel it.

**Kodak:** It's a studio, Static. The power's fine. We've got a generator.

**Static:** The generator's in the Park, Kodak. The one that set fire to the zoo.

**Kodak:** It didn't set fire to the zoo, mate. It set fire to a bin.

**Static:** A bin's nearly a zoo, Kodak.

**Lux:** It's not a zoo. It's a bin with feelings.

**Static:** Thanks, Lux. That's very comforting.

*A sudden crackle. The lamp flickers. The red glow of the boombox dims. Everyone freezes.*

**Static:** Oh no. Oh no no no.

**Nova:** Static, breathe. It's a flicker.

**Static:** It's not a flicker, Nova. It's the start of a blackout. I can hear it.

**Kodak:** You can hear a blackout?

**Static:** I can hear the power thinking about it, Kodak. Ok.

*Lux slowly sits up from the beanbag, holding a single bottle cap in each hand, like a pair of cymbals.*

**Lux:** It's okay, Static. I've got the shiny ones. They'll light the way.

**Static:** The shiny ones don't light anything, Lux.

**Lux:** They do if you believe in them, Static. Look!

*She holds the bottle caps up. The lamp flickers again. The light catches the caps, and for a moment they throw small gold reflections across the walls.*

**Kodak:** Hold still. Got it.

*Kodak takes a photo. The flash goes off. The studio is lit for a split second, and everyone is frozen in the shimmer of gold.*

**Nova:** That's beautiful, Lux.

**Lux:** It's a treasure, Nova. I told you.

**Static:** Okay. Okay. The lights are fine. I'm fine.

*The lamp steadies. The boombox glows red again. Static takes a deep breath, removes his headphones, and smiles weakly.*

**Static:** Thanks, Lux. You're a good treasure.

**Lux:** You're a good DJ, Static. Even when you're scared.

**Static:** Especially when I'm scared, Lux.

*Nova stands up and goes to the window. Outside, the SALT is quiet. A single streetlight hums. A flash of red appears at the far end of the street, then vanishes.*

**Nova:** Kodak, did you see that?

**Kodak:** See what?

**Nova:** A light. Red. By the dairy.

**Kodak:** It's probably the Riccarton Boys.

**Nova:** Probably. But they've got a lot of lollies, and nobody's watching the dairy.

**Kodak:** Nova, you're worrying too much.

**Nova:** Someone has to, Kodak. That's what I do.

## Scene 5: The Dairy, Midnight

*Quiet. Ruth stands behind the counter, a cup of tea in one hand and a tin of biscuits in the other. The window is dark except for the neon sign reading "DAIRY, OPEN 24HRS". Outside, a single car rolls slowly past, headlights off. Ruth watches it go.*

**Ruth:** Right. Nobody's getting my biscuits tonight.

*The bell above the door rings. Ruth spins round, hand on the tin. The Night Kiwi stands in the doorway, dripping wet, mask on, jacket soaked. He holds up a hand.*

**Night Kiwi:** Don't panic, Ruth. It's just me.

**Ruth:** Just you? You're dripping on my lino, bro.

**Night Kiwi:** It's raining in the SALT. The Nor'wester's gone to sleep and left the drizzle behind.

**Ruth:** You could have come through the front door like a normal person.

**Night Kiwi:** I've never been normal, Ruth. It's my brand.

**Ruth:** Kiwi, what do you want?

**Night Kiwi:** I want to warn you.

**Ruth:** About what?

**Night Kiwi:** The Riccarton Boys are coming here tonight. They're going to steal your biscuits, and then they're going to park their hatchback across your bins.

**Ruth:** Thanks, Kiwi. I've already had that happen this week.

**Night Kiwi:** Then you're ahead of the game, Ruth. Most people are behind.

**Ruth:** Why are you helping me?

**Night Kiwi:** Because Vito's tomato painting is back on the wall, and it's not going to stay there. And the Riccarton Boys have decided the dairy is the next target.

**Ruth:** Why?

**Night Kiwi:** Because it's the only place in the SALT where the lollies and the hubcaps can meet. Everyone's been trading here for years.

**Ruth:** I didn't know that.

**Night Kiwi:** Nobody does, Ruth. That's the beauty of a dairy. It's a secret market, disguised as milk.

*Ruth pulls a stool across the floor and sits down heavily.*

**Ruth:** Okay, Kiwi. What do I do?

**Night Kiwi:** You do what you've always done. Sell milk. Serve chips. Keep the lights on.

**Ruth:** And the Riccarton Boys?

**Night Kiwi:** You make them pay for the biscuits.

*Ruth laughs, a short, tired laugh.*

**Ruth:** You're mental, you know that?

**Night Kiwi:** It's a statement, Ruth. Please don't ask.

*The Night Kiwi moves to the door, pauses, and looks back over his shoulder.*

**Night Kiwi:** Oh, and Ruth. The best biscuit in the SALT is the one nobody wants. Keep it for the Don.

**Ruth:** Why?

**Night Kiwi:** Because he'll eat it, Ruth. He always does.

*He slips out. The bell rings. Ruth stares at the closed door, then slowly reaches into the tin and pulls out the stalest biscuit in the box.*

**Ruth:** Right. Vito's getting this one, then.

## End of Part 6

**Story threads moved forward:**

- Mr Dwyer has told Jace the cops will catch the Riccarton Boys eventually. Jace has been sacked from the showroom, or at least told to stop hanging around it.
- Ruth has sent Brooke packing and found herself at the centre of a secret market. She has also been warned by the Night Kiwi that the Riccarton Boys are coming for the dairy.
- The Velvet hatchback, with its gold tomato sticker, has been stolen from the gala, and Sal is said to be driving it. Pip is writing it all down.
- Static's studio blackout was narrowly avoided, thanks to Lux and a pair of bottle caps. Nova spotted a red light by the dairy.
- The Night Kiwi has warned Ruth, and given her one piece of advice: keep the stalest biscuit for the Don.
