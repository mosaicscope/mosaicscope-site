# MosaicScope Story, Part 9a: The Unmasking

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 9 is split into 9a and 9b to keep each file manageable.

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 8:** The gold Roma painting is going to the Market. Kyle has until Friday to recover four hubcaps from the duck pond and the bin. Pip is investigating the Night Kiwi's hat. Bill runs a pie stall under the Night Kiwi's name. Vito has left a note in the biscuit tin.

---

## Scene 1: The Market, Friday Morning

*The Market is busy. Sun is out, a light sea breeze blows off the Port Hills, and the pav stall has a new sign: "PAV WITH A ROMA: SPECIAL TRIBUTE." A crowd has gathered around a large gold-framed painting leaning against the stall. Velvet stands beside it with a champagne glass. Mikey Stone holds a velvet rope and looks grumpy about the crowd.*

**Velvet:** Friends! Citizens of the SALT! Today the painting of the Roma goes to the people!

**Mikey:** Sir, it's a bit windy for a speech.

**Velvet:** Mikey, darling, every speech is a bit windy. That's the point.

**Tiles (from the crowd, gold jacket on):** Ponsonby! Are we getting a lolly?

**Velvet:** Tiles, you'll get a lolly if you stop heckling.

**Tiles:** I'm not heckling, Ponsonby. I'm cheering. Look, the crowd's doing a wave.

*A half-hearted wave ripples through the crowd. A pigeon flaps off a sign.*

**Velvet:** Splendid. Now, the painting. It's a gift to the Market, from me, to celebrate the most honest tomato in the South Island.

**Vito (from the back of the crowd, pinstripes immaculate):** Ponsonby. You're giving my tomato away?

**Velvet:** Vittorio! Darling! I'm giving it to the people. The most generous thing I've ever done.

**Vito:** It's a painting of my tomato, Ponsonby. You can't eat it.

**Velvet:** That's what makes it art, Vittorio.

**Vito:** That's what makes it a waste of gold leaf.

*Vito takes a pineapple lolly from his pocket, unwraps it, and pops it in his mouth.*

**Vito:** There. We're even.

**Velvet:** We're even, Vittorio. Thank you.

*Ruth pushes through the crowd, dairy apron on, a paper bag in one hand.*

**Ruth:** Morning, Vito. Morning, Ponsonby. I've brought biscuits.

**Vito:** Ruth! A biscuit! Is it stale?

**Ruth:** It's fresh, Vito. For once.

**Vito:** Madonna. A fresh biscuit. I'm moved.

**Ruth:** Don't get used to it, bro.

**Vito:** Ruth, this is very generous.

**Ruth:** It's not generous, Vito. It's a deal. You take the biscuit. I keep the dairy.

**Vito:** A fair deal. Sal, write it down.

**Sal:** Don, I'm writing it on a napkin.

**Vito:** That's a very serious napkin, Sal.

## Scene 2: The Pav Stall, Mid-Morning

*The pav stall is crowded. Sal is behind the counter in a paper hat, looking uncomfortable. Honey stands at the front of the queue with a clipboard, directing traffic.*

**Honey:** One pav, one Roma, no lollies. Next!

**Sal:** Honey, why are you running the queue?

**Honey:** Because you've been counting the same pav for ten minutes, Sal.

**Sal:** I'm counting carefully. The Don says I should be careful with the tomatoes.

**Honey:** The Don's not running the stall, Sal. I am.

**Gurjit (from next door):** Honey! Tell the crowd my pizza's not a crime!

**Honey:** Gurjit, your pizza's on the list, under "Pineapple, Contentious."

**Gurjit:** That's not a crime, Honey. That's a lifestyle.

**Honey:** It's a lifestyle that needs a tag, Gurjit. Sorry.

*Priya walks up, laptop bag over her shoulder.*

**Priya:** Sal, can I have a pav with a Roma?

**Sal:** It's a special tribute, love. Eight dollars.

**Priya:** Eight dollars for a tomato?

**Sal:** It's a painted tomato, love. The price is for the art.

*Sal wraps a pav in a napkin with a single small Roma on top. Priya takes a bite, chews slowly.*

**Priya:** Sal, this is actually good.

**Sal:** My nonna makes the sauce, love.

**Priya:** The sauce is on the pav?

**Sal:** The sauce is on everything, love. That's why it's the Famiglia.

*A commotion at the far end of the Market. A shout, a clatter, and a splash.*

**Honey:** Oh no. Something's in the pond.

**Priya:** That's not the pond, Honey. That's the Park.

**Honey:** The Park's got a pond, Priya. It's on the list.

## Scene 3: Duck Pond, Mid-Morning

*The duck pond in the Park. Sunlight dances on the water. Kyle is waist-deep, holding a hubcap above his head. Beside him, in a pair of old togs, Mo the busker wades slowly, guitar case strapped to his back. Ducks are everywhere, looking furious.*

**Kyle:** Mo, mate, I've got one! Hubcap number one!

**Mo:** Brilliant, Kyle! Now let's get the other three!

**Kyle:** I can't see the others, mate. It's all weed and ducks.

**Mo:** Ducks don't like hubcaps, Kyle. They see them as competition.

*A large white duck waddles up to Kyle, neck extended, hissing. Kyle steps back and nearly loses his footing.*

**Kyle:** Mo, the duck's looking at me.

**Mo:** Because you're holding its hubcap, mate.

**Kyle:** It's not its hubcap, Mo! It's Mr Dwyer's!

**Mo:** Tell the duck that, Kyle. It's not listening.

*Pip walks along the bank, eating a sausage roll.*

**Pip:** Kyle, what are you doing in the duck pond?

**Kyle:** Recovering hubcaps, officer!

**Pip:** Why are they in the duck pond?

**Kyle:** Because the decoy drove into it, officer. And the Riccarton Boys threw one in on the way out.

**Pip:** Why would they throw a hubcap in a pond?

**Kyle:** It was a community hubcap, officer. Meant to be shared.

**Pip:** Kyle, that's the most ridiculous thing I've heard all week.

*Mo wades over, holding a second hubcap, dripping weed.*

**Mo:** Got number two, Kyle!

**Kyle:** That's two!

**Mo:** And here's a duck holding a third one.

**Kyle:** That's not a hubcap, Mo. That's a bread roll.

**Mo:** It's a very convincing bread roll, mate.

*The duck drops the bread roll, steps forward, and flaps aggressively.*

**Mo:** Oh, wait. That's not a roll.

*A sudden splash behind them. A figure in a dark jacket rises out of the water like a seal. He pushes a kiwi-bird mask up and gasps for breath.*

**Kyle:** Who's that?

**Pip:** That's the Night Kiwi, Kyle.

**Mo:** The Night Kiwi's in the pond?

**Pip:** He's been in the pond the whole time, Mo. Watching the ducks.

*He wades out and sits on the bank, dripping. He takes the mask off completely and places it on his knee. He's a young man, mid-twenties, freckled, grinning.*

**Night Kiwi:** Morning, Pip. Morning, Kyle. Morning, the entire duck population.

**Pip:** Who are you?

**Night Kiwi:** I'm the rumour, Pip. You've been looking for me for a week.

**Pip:** You're just a bloke in a pond.

**Night Kiwi:** A bloke with a very good hat.

**Pip:** The hat's in an evidence bag, mate.

**Night Kiwi:** Then you've got my hat and a very confused duck.

**Night Kiwi:** Right. My name's Hemi.

**Pip:** Hemi what?

**Hemi:** Hemi Walker. I'm a surveyor for the council. I've been on the Cathedral renovation for eleven years.

**Pip:** The one that's still under renovation?

**Hemi:** That's the one. It's a very slow surveyor's job.

**Pip:** Why dress as a rumour?

**Hemi:** Because nobody listens to a surveyor, Pip. They do listen to a kiwi.

**Kyle:** So you got the lights back on at Velvet's gala?

**Hemi:** That was me, Kyle. I was on the gallery. Good view of the switchboard.

**Mo:** And the hubcaps?

**Hemi:** The Riccarton Boys kept taking them. I kept putting them in the pond. It was the only place they'd stay still.

**Pip:** Why keep watching the Famiglia?

**Hemi:** Because somebody had to, Pip. Vito's got eyes on the whole SALT. I was making sure the eyes were fair.

## Scene 4: The Market, Afternoon: The Unmasking

*Lunch rush. The Velvet painting is propped against a stall, gold catching the sun. Bill sits by his pie stall. Priya is writing. Hemi sits on a folding chair beside Bill, wrapped in a blanket, hair drying in the breeze.*

**Bill:** Hemi, lad, you look terrible.

**Hemi:** Thanks, Bill. I've been in the duck pond.

**Bill:** Ducks have no respect for a bloke's dignity, son.

**Priya:** Hemi, can I ask why you pretended to be the Night Kiwi?

**Hemi:** Because nobody looks at the SALT unless there's a gang in it, or a painting, or a tomato. So I gave them a rumour to talk about.

**Priya:** And talking's how people start to care?

**Hemi:** That's it, Priya. Talking's how a place stays alive.

*Ruth walks up with a tray of biscuits and the dairy tin under one arm.*

**Ruth:** Hemi, you're soaking.

**Hemi:** I'm a sponge of the SALT, Ruth.

**Ruth:** Did you tell the Don?

**Hemi:** Not yet. I was going to tell him after the painting's gone.

**Ruth:** He's going to take it personally, Hemi.

**Hemi:** He's already taken it personally, Ruth. He sent me a biscuit tin.

*Vito walks up, Sal behind him. He looks Hemi up and down.*

**Vito:** So you're the Night Kiwi.

**Hemi:** I'm the rumour, Vito. The Night Kiwi's a statement.

**Vito:** A statement with a very nice hat.

**Hemi:** It's in an evidence bag, Vito.

**Vito:** Then I'll make a new one. A tomato hat.

*Vito pulls a pineapple lolly from his pocket and holds it out. Hemi takes it.*

**Vito:** For the rumour. For keeping an eye on my tomatoes.

**Hemi:** Thanks, Vito.

**Vito:** Thank Sal, Hemi. He's been watching the ducks since Tuesday.

**Sal:** Don, I've been watching the ducks.

**Vito:** Ducks are just small rumours with wings, Sal.

*Velvet strolls up, champagne in hand.*

**Velvet:** Hemi! My darling surveyor! I've always wanted to meet the Night Kiwi.

**Hemi:** Ponsonby, you're in my photos.

**Velvet:** Darling, I'm always in photos. Survey my painting instead. It's gold, and terribly expensive.

*Hemi stands, walks to the painting, and runs one finger along the frame.*

**Hemi:** It's not gold, Ponsonby.

**Velvet:** Of course it's gold. I paid for the gold leaf.

**Hemi:** The leaf's on the frame. The tomato's painted. It's gold paint.

**Velvet:** That's still gold, darling.

**Hemi:** It's a very good imitation. The lid's still got the sticker on it.

*Hemi lifts a corner of the canvas to show a tin lid, with a sticker reading "MITRE 10: GOLD METALLIC."*

**Velvet:** Darling, I'd never!

*Silence. Velvet looks at the sticker, then at Vito. Vito looks at the sticker, then at Velvet. They both start laughing, hard.*

**Vito:** Ponsonby. You've been fooled by a tin of paint.

**Velvet:** Vittorio, it's the most expensive tin of paint in Canterbury!

**Vito:** Then you're the most expensive fool in Canterbury.

**Velvet:** And you're the most expensive tomato, Vittorio!

*Vito laughs so hard he has to hold onto Sal. Ruth shakes her head, smiling. Gurjit shouts from the next stall.*

**Gurjit:** Is it a pizza? Can I have a pizza?

**Ruth:** It's a tin of paint, Gurjit.

**Gurjit:** Then it's a pizza with paint on it, Ruth. That's the best pizza yet!

## End of Part 9a

**Threads moved forward:**

- The Velvet painting is revealed as gold paint from a hardware shop. Vito and Velvet are now unlikely friends.
- The Night Kiwi is Hemi Walker, a council surveyor on the Cathedral renovation. He invented the rumour to get the SALT talked about.
- Kyle recovered two hubcaps from the duck pond, with help from Mo and a determined duck. Two more are still missing.
