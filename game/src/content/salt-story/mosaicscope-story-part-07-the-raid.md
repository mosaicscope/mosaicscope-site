# MosaicScope Story, Part 7: The Raid

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1: "The Cold Snap." Part 2: "The Nor'wester." Part 3: "The Morning After." Part 4: "The Meeting." Part 5: "The Heist." Part 6: "The Revenge."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 6:** The Night Kiwi has warned Ruth the Riccarton Boys are raiding the dairy tonight. Ruth has kept the stalest biscuit for Vito. Sal is said to be driving the stolen Velvet hatchback. Pip is writing everything down.

---

## Scene 1: The Dairy, Early Evening

*The SALT at dusk. Shopfronts are lighting up, the neon sign of the dairy buzzes "DAIRY, OPEN 24HRS". Ruth has pushed the milk crates into a rough barricade by the door. Gurjit stands on a step ladder, adjusting his pizza sign. Honey sits on the counter with a clipboard, colour-coding a list of names.*

**Ruth:** Honey, why are you on my counter?

**Honey:** Because it's the best view in the SALT, Ruth. You can see the whole street, the bins, and the Riccarton Boys coming.

**Ruth:** You're not supposed to sit on the counter, Honey. It's a food surface.

**Honey:** Everything's a food surface if you're hungry enough.

**Gurjit:** Ruth, I've got a problem with the sign.

**Ruth:** Gurjit, the sign's fine. It's been fine for years.

**Gurjit:** It's been fine, but it's a bit crooked now. Look.

*Gurjit points at the sign. It's clearly tilted sideways, the "PIZZA" lit up in a lopsided way.*

**Ruth:** That's not crooked, bro. That's rustic.

**Gurjit:** Rustic doesn't sell pizza, Ruth.

**Honey:** It sells a lot of pizza in the SALT, Gurjit. Trust me. I've got the numbers.

**Gurjit:** You've got the numbers?

**Honey:** I've got colour-coded numbers, Gurjit. It's a very strong argument.

*Priya walks up the footpath, notebook under her arm, bow tie hanging loose. She stops by the door and peers in.*

**Priya:** Is this where the big fight is?

**Ruth:** There's no fight, love. There's a dairy.

**Priya:** Kiwi said the fight starts here. He said it on the roof.

**Ruth:** Kiwi says a lot of things on roofs, bro. Most of them are rubbish.

**Priya:** I'm writing about it, though. For my essay. Can I come in?

**Ruth:** Come in, but sit down and don't write about the pizza.

**Priya:** Why not?

**Ruth:** Because the pizza's got pineapple, and that's a whole other essay, love.

## Scene 2: Riccarton Road, Dusk

*Engines rumble in the dark. Five cars roll along Riccarton Road with their lights off, bass low and rattling. In the lead car, a yellow hatchback with a patched bonnet, sits Tanner, at the wheel, grinning. Beside him is Brooke, holding a clipboard of his own with the words "THE RAID" written on it in marker.*

**Tanner:** Brooke, you're making that clipboard look dodgy.

**Brooke:** It's a plan, Tanner. Plans are supposed to look like a bit of a mess.

**Tanner:** Okay. What's step one?

**Brooke:** Step one: take the dairy's biscuits.

**Tanner:** Step two?

**Brooke:** Step two: park the hatchback across Ruth's bins.

**Tanner:** Step three?

**Brooke:** Step three: nobody knows what step three is, Tanner.

**Tanner:** That's not a plan, bro. That's a holiday.

*In the second car, Jace sits in the passenger seat, feet on the dash, cap low.*

**Jace:** Tanner! Stop talking and drive, mate!

**Tanner:** I'm driving, Jace. I'm the best driver in Riccarton.

**Jace:** You're the only driver in Riccarton who thinks he's the best, Tanner.

**Tanner:** Don't be jealous, Jace. You've got the cap.

*Behind them, the third car's engine sputters and dies. A young bloke in a black hoodie jumps out and kicks the bumper.*

**Hoodie Bloke:** Oi! It's dead again!

**Jace:** Did you put the petrol in, Ricky?

**Ricky:** I put the lollies in, Jace! I thought they'd make it go!

**Jace:** Lollies don't make cars go, Ricky. They make them sticky.

**Ricky:** That's why it's stuck! It's full of pineapple!

*Pip's patrol car slides silently into the street behind them, lights off. Pip is driving, Hobbs is in the passenger seat with a pie on his knee.*

**Hobbs:** Pip, turn the lights on.

**Pip:** I'm trying, Sarge. I'm being stealthy.

**Hobbs:** You're being a bit stealthy with a siren on the roof.

**Pip:** The siren's a decoy, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** A siren's not a decoy, Pip. It's a siren.

*Pip flips on the lights. Blue and red wash over the street. The dead car's hoodie bloke freezes. Tanner's hatchback brakes so hard that the lead car's bonnet swings open.*

**Pip (over the loudspeaker):** Riccarton Boys! Pull over!

**Tanner:** Oh, bro. It's Pip!

**Brooke:** Keep driving, Tanner!

**Tanner:** I can't, bro! The bonnet's up!

**Brooke:** Then drive with your head out the window!

**Tanner:** That's not how a car works, Brooke!

**Jace:** Tanner! Just drive into the dairy!

**Tanner:** Why?

**Jace:** Because it's got the biscuits, mate!

## Scene 3: The Dairy Car Park, Moments Later

*Tanner's hatchback comes screeching into the dairy's car park, bonnet still bouncing open, hubcaps spinning. It jerks to a stop beside the bins. Brooke leaps out, clipboard in hand.*

**Brooke:** Right, lads! Operation Biscuit is go!

*The other cars pile in behind. Jace jumps from the second car and strides up the footpath toward the dairy door. Ricky kicks his dead car, then sprints after him.*

**Ruth (through the window):** Oi! Jace! Get off my bins!

**Jace:** Morning, Ruth! Lovely evening for a raid!

**Ruth:** It's not morning, bro. It's night. And it's not a raid. It's a dairy.

**Jace:** It's a dairy with biscuits, Ruth. That's a raid.

*Brooke starts pulling milk crates out of the barricade, tossing them onto the pavement. Gurjit climbs down off his ladder, holding a pizza box like a shield.*

**Gurjit:** Stop that! You'll ruin my pizza!

**Brooke:** Pineapple's a crime, bro.

**Gurjit:** Pineapple's a choice, Brooke! And I stand by it!

*Honey hops off the counter, clipboard raised, and marches to the door.*

**Honey:** Brooke! That's a red zone. Everything you're touching is red.

**Brooke:** I'm not touching anything, Honey. I'm just holding.

**Honey:** You're holding a milk crate, Brooke. That's touching.

*Tanner strides to the front of the hatchback, tracksuit flapping, and raises both arms.*

**Tanner:** Riccarton Boys! We've taken the dairy!

**Priya (from inside, mildly):** You haven't taken the dairy. You're standing in the car park.

**Tanner:** It's a symbolic taking, love.

**Priya:** It's a very confusing taking.

*Ruth steps out the front door, holding the dairy tin above her head like a trophy.*

**Ruth:** Right. Listen up, you lot. Nobody's getting the biscuits.

**Tanner:** Ruth, you can't stop us, bro. We've got the parking.

**Ruth:** You don't have the parking, Tanner. You've got a hatchback with its bonnet up.

**Tanner:** It's a very dramatic hatchback, Ruth.

**Ruth:** It's a bloody liability, Tanner.

---

## Scene 4: The Standoff

*Under the dairy's neon sign. Ruth stands in the doorway. Behind her, Gurjit, Honey and Priya are crowded at the window. In front of her, five Riccarton Boys in tracksuits spread across the car park. Pip and Hobbs have stepped out of the patrol car at the edge of the lot. Hobbs is still holding his pie.*

**Hobbs:** Right. Evening, all.

**Tanner:** Evening, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** What's going on here?

**Brooke:** It's a community biscuit trade, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** It's a crime scene, Brooke. There's a bin lid on the footpath.

**Brooke:** That's a community bin lid, Sarge.

**Pip:** Brooke, you're holding a milk crate.

**Brooke:** It's a community milk crate, officer.

**Pip:** Put it down.

**Brooke:** Yes, officer.

*Brooke puts the milk crate down very carefully, then stands on it.*

**Hobbs:** Brooke, get off the crate.

**Brooke:** It's a tall crate, Sarge. I'm making a statement.

**Hobbs:** Your statement's a bit wobbly, mate.

*Jace steps out from behind a bin, wiping his nose with the back of his hand. He straightens up and looks at Ruth.*

**Jace:** Ruth. Be reasonable. The Riccarton Boys want the parking. You've got a dairy full of biscuits. It's a fair trade.

**Ruth:** It's not a fair trade, Jace. You're taking my biscuits and my bins for a car park you don't even own.

**Jace:** We'll own it, Ruth. Eventually.

**Ruth:** Eventually's not today, bro.

**Jace:** Then give us the biscuits now, and we'll go.

**Ruth:** And if I don't?

**Jace:** Then Tanner's going to park the hatchback across your door, and nobody's going to get in or out.

**Tanner:** I'll do it, Ruth! I've got the keys!

**Ruth:** You've got the keys to a car with the bonnet up, Tanner.

**Tanner:** It's still a car, Ruth.

*Ruth steps forward, one hand on the doorframe, dairy tin in the other. She looks hard at Jace.*

**Ruth:** Jace. Do you know what the dairy's for?

**Jace:** Milk and chips, Ruth.

**Ruth:** No. It's for the whole SALT. It's where the lollies go, and the hubcaps, and the pizza, and the bins. It's where people come when nobody else is open.

**Jace:** That's a lot of responsibility for a corner shop, Ruth.

**Ruth:** It's the most responsibility a corner shop's ever had, bro.

---

## Scene 5: The Fight

*Something snaps. Tanner lunges forward, arms out, aiming for the open biscuit tin. Ruth swings the tin up to block him. Biscuits fly. Brooke jumps off his milk crate and grabs a foam sword from the back of the hatchback, swinging wildly. Flash bursts through the hedge at the side of the car park, brandishing a second foam sword, shouting.*

**Flash:** Who wants it?!

**Brooke:** Oi! Flash! That's our biscuit!

**Flash:** Nobody's biscuit, Brooke! It's the Mosaics' biscuit!

**Ruby (from the hedge):** It's Ruth's biscuit, Flash! Don't be a menace!

**Flash:** I'm not a menace, Ruby! I'm a legend!

*Ruby leaps out of the hedge, grabs Flash by the back of his shirt, and yanks him backward. They both stumble and fall into a bin.*

**Ruby:** You're a menace, Flash! You always are!

**Flash:** You're a menace, Ruby! Your serve's wonky!

*Tiles appears at the edge of the car park, wearing his shiny gold jacket. He's holding a bottle of pineapple lolly juice like a sword.*

**Tiles:** Right! Party's on! Who's up for a dairy brawl?

**Glitter (from the window):** Tiles! It's not a party! It's a fight!

**Tiles:** Glitter, every fight's a party if you do it right!

**Nova (appearing beside Glitter, holding a first aid kit):** Everyone, please stop! Nobody needs to get hurt!

**Tanner:** I'm not hurting anyone, Nova! I'm just waving my arms!

**Nova:** Then stop waving them, Tanner. You'll hit someone.

**Tanner:** I'm not going to hit someone, Nova. I'm going to hit the bin.

*Tanner swings at the bin. The lid clatters off and bounces across the car park, spinning in circles like a wobbly coin. It skids to a stop at Pip's feet.*

**Pip:** Tanner. That's a bin lid.

**Tanner:** It's a community bin lid, officer.

**Pip:** Tanner, it's a bin lid. And it's come from my bin.

*Lux comes sprinting out from behind the dairy, scooter bumping over the kerb, a bottle cap held high above her head.*

**Lux:** Found a gold one! Found a gold one!

**Brooke:** Lux, not now!

**Lux:** It's a treasure, Brooke! It's the best treasure in the car park!

*She zooms in a wide circle, scooter wobbling, and nearly runs straight over Hobbs' pie.*

**Hobbs:** Lux! The pie!

**Lux:** Sorry, Sarge! It's an emergency pie!

**Hobbs:** Every pie is an emergency, Lux.

---

## Scene 6: The Biscuit Truce

*The fight is winding down. Foam swords are lying across the car park. Two Riccarton Boys are sitting on the kerb with their heads in their hands. Flash and Ruby are side by side on the bin, sharing a pineapple lolly. Tanner is sitting on the bonnet of his hatchback, hubcaps spinning slowly on the ground beside him.*

*Ruth walks slowly out into the middle of the car park. The dairy tin is still in her hand. She stops, looks at the mess, and sighs.*

**Ruth:** Right. Everybody sit down.

**Tanner:** Ruth, we can't sit. We're in a car park.

**Ruth:** Then sit on the car, Tanner. Everybody's got a car.

*Slowly, the Riccarton Boys shuffle over. Brooke gets off the milk crate. Jace takes his cap off and holds it in his hands.*

**Ruth:** Now. I've got a biscuit. It's the stalest one in the tin.

**Jace:** Ruth, that's not a fair trade.

**Ruth:** It's the only trade I've got, Jace.

*She takes out a biscuit, holds it up. It's grey around the edges and slightly cracked.*

**Ruth:** This is the Don's biscuit. He said he wanted it. It's historic.

**Jace:** Historic?

**Ruth:** It's been in the tin since the earthquake, bro.

**Tanner:** Okay, Ruth. We'll share it.

**Ruth:** You don't share a stale biscuit, Tanner. You bloody well eat it.

*Tanner takes it. He bites. There's a long pause. Everyone watches.*

**Tanner:** Ruth. That's the worst biscuit I've ever had.

**Ruth:** I know, bro. That's why I gave it to you.

*Flash starts laughing. Ruby elbows him, then starts laughing too. Glitter appears in the window with her phone, filming. Honey writes something on her clipboard.*

**Honey:** Riccarton Boys: one stale biscuit, one hubcap, one bin lid, zero parking. Red zone resolved.

**Jace:** Honey, mate, we're not resolved. We're just tired.

**Honey:** That's a resolution, Jace. Congratulations.

*Pip walks slowly over to the bin lid and picks it up. She turns it over in her hands.*

**Pip:** Brooke, Tanner, Jace. You're all going to need to come down to the station tomorrow. Bring a biscuit.

**Jace:** A biscuit, officer?

**Pip:** It's a fine, Jace. Lots of them are biscuits now.

*Jace puts his cap back on. He sighs, long and deep, and looks at Ruth.*

**Jace:** Ruth. Fair play. You've got a good dairy.

**Ruth:** I know, bro. It's the only one left in the SALT with the lights on.

*A silence. Then a car horn sounds from the far end of the street. A battered grey sedan rolls to a stop at the kerb. The window winds down. It's Sal, driving the stolen Velvet hatchback's replacement, a sensible grey station wagon. Vito sits in the passenger seat, pinstripes immaculate, holding a pineapple lolly.*

**Vito:** Ruth! Ruth! I heard there was a fight!

**Ruth:** There was, Vito. But it's sorted.

**Vito:** Sorted? Madonna. You had a fight and you didn't tell me?

**Ruth:** You weren't invited, Vito.

**Vito:** I'm always invited, Ruth. I'm the Don.

**Ruth:** You're the Don of a lolly, Vito. Here.

*She holds out the empty biscuit tin, now dusted with crumbs.*

**Vito:** An empty tin? A gift?

**Ruth:** It's a sign, Vito. You've had the best biscuit in the SALT, and now it's gone.

**Vito:** Gone? Ruth, that's the saddest thing I've ever heard.

**Ruth:** It's the most honest thing you'll hear tonight, bro.

*Vito stares at the tin. Slowly, he reaches out, takes it, and holds it to his chest like a precious jewel. He looks at Sal.*

**Vito:** Sal. We're going home.

**Sal:** Yes, Don.

**Vito:** And, Sal. Next time we visit the dairy, we bring our own biscuits.

**Sal:** Yes, Don. We'll bring a whole box.

## End of Part 7

**Story threads moved forward:**

- The Riccarton Boys' raid on the dairy ended in a biscuit truce. Pip has told Jace, Tanner and Brooke to come to the station tomorrow with a biscuit.
- Ruth has held the dairy and won respect from the Boys, and from Vito.
- Vito now has the empty biscuit tin, which he treats as a precious jewel.
- Sal is driving a replacement station wagon, after the stolen Velvet hatchback was recovered.
- Flash and Ruby are back on friendly terms, sharing a lolly on the bin.
- Priya has a fight scene for her essay, and a bin lid that Pip is now holding as evidence.

**Next:** Part 8 covers the station, where the Riccarton Boys have to front up with biscuits. It also covers Hobbs' break, Pip's investigation into the Night Kiwi, and a surprise visit from Mr Dwyer, who wants his hubcaps back.
