# MosaicScope Story, Part 9b: The Invitation

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. This is the second half of Part 9.

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 9a:** The Velvet painting is revealed as Mitre 10 gold paint. Hemi Walker is unmasked as the Night Kiwi, a council surveyor. Kyle has recovered two of four hubcaps from the duck pond.

---

## Scene 1: Riccarton Road, Friday Evening

*Late sun on the dealerships. The Riccarton Motors sign has stopped flickering and reads "RICCARTON MOTORS" in clean white letters. Kyle stands on the forecourt with four hubcaps lined up on the tarmac. Two are shining. One is bent. One still has a duck's footprint on it. Mr Dwyer stands beside him, sunglasses on, arms folded.*

**Mr Dwyer:** Four hubcaps, Kyle. By Friday.

**Kyle:** It's Friday, boss. Just before six.

**Mr Dwyer:** Good. Are they all here?

**Kyle:** Two are, boss. Two are on the tarmac. One's bent, one's got a duck on it.

**Mr Dwyer:** A duck, Kyle?

**Kyle:** It's a very determined duck, boss. Mo says it's got a future in hubcaps.

**Mr Dwyer:** Mo's a busker, Kyle.

**Kyle:** He's a busker in a wetsuit, boss. That's a different job.

*Jace walks up the road, cap pulled low, hands in his pockets. He stops at the edge of the forecourt and looks at the hubcaps.*

**Jace:** Mr Dwyer. Can I have a job?

**Mr Dwyer:** Jace. You've been fired since Tuesday.

**Jace:** I know, boss. I've been thinking about it. I'd like to be a hubcap manager.

**Mr Dwyer:** A hubcap manager?

**Jace:** Yeah, boss. I'll keep the hubcaps out of the bins and the duck pond. And I'll stop Tanner and Brooke trading them for biscuits.

**Mr Dwyer:** That's a very specific job, Jace.

**Jace:** It's a very specific problem, boss.

**Mr Dwyer:** Fine. You've got a month. If another hubcap goes missing, you're on a traffic cone for the rest of your life.

**Jace:** Fair enough, boss. Deal.

*Mr Dwyer holds out his hand. Jace shakes it, then turns to Kyle.*

**Jace:** Kyle, mate. You're my assistant.

**Kyle:** I'm fired, Jace.

**Jace:** You're rehired, bro. As an assistant.

**Kyle:** Do I get a raise?

**Jace:** You get a pineapple lolly, Kyle. It's the best raise in the SALT.

**Kyle:** That's not a raise, Jace.

**Jace:** It's a lolly with prospects, Kyle.

## Scene 2: The Dairy, Late Friday

*Night has come to the SALT. The dairy neon sign buzzes "DAIRY, OPEN 24HRS". Ruth is behind the counter counting her takings. The Velvet painting, now with its Market sticker, leans against the window. Hemi sits on a stool by the door, wrapped in a blanket, drinking hot chocolate. Priya sits beside him with her notebook open.*

**Ruth:** Hemi, another hot chocolate?

**Hemi:** Yeah, Ruth. And a biscuit. A stale one.

**Ruth:** I've only got fresh ones, bro.

**Hemi:** Then give me the fresh one. I'm not fussy.

**Ruth:** You're fussy about biscuits, Hemi.

**Hemi:** I'm fussy about the right biscuits, Ruth. The stale ones are a statement.

**Ruth:** You're a bit of a statement yourself, bro.

*Priya looks up from her notebook.*

**Priya:** Hemi, can I ask one more thing?

**Hemi:** Go on, Priya. It's the last question tonight.

**Priya:** What's the SALT, really?

**Hemi:** It's a corner, Priya. Four streets, one dairy, one stage, one casino, and a lot of people who don't know where else to go.

**Priya:** That's not very exciting.

**Hemi:** It's the best corner in Christchurch. Everybody in this town passes through it eventually. Whether they're buying milk, parking the car, or nicking a tomato.

**Priya:** That's really nice, Hemi.

**Hemi:** It's the truth. The rest is biscuits.

*The bell rings. Glitter bursts in, phone in hand, grinning.*

**Glitter:** Hemi! Everyone's talking about you! The whole SALT's gone mad!

**Hemi:** About what, Glitter?

**Glitter:** The Night Kiwi! Surveyor, lolly from the Don, the lights back on at the gala. It's a whole legend now.

**Hemi:** That's not news, Glitter. That's gossip.

**Glitter:** Gossip with a lolly, Hemi. That's the best kind!

**Ruth:** Glitter, you're shaking the lino.

**Glitter:** I'm excited, Ruth! Tiles is throwing a party tomorrow night. At the stage. For the Night Kiwi!

**Hemi:** A party for me?

**Glitter:** For the rumour, Hemi. It's a whole thing. You're invited.

**Hemi:** Glitter, I'm not very good at parties.

**Glitter:** Nobody's good at parties, babe. That's why they're fun.

*Hemi looks at the painting, then at the window, then back at Ruth. He smiles, a little shy.*

**Hemi:** Okay. I'll come. But I'm bringing a biscuit.

**Ruth:** A stale one, Hemi. Don't argue.

**Hemi:** Ruth, I'd never argue with a biscuit.

*Glitter spins round and bursts out the door, shouting to the street.*

**Glitter (outside):** Tiles! He's coming! The Night Kiwi's coming!

**Tiles (distant):** Brilliant! Tell him to bring a foam sword!

**Hemi:** I'm not bringing a foam sword.

**Ruth:** You're bringing a biscuit, Hemi. That's enough.

## Scene 3: The Stage, Saturday Afternoon

*The stage behind the Market is being decorated. String lights are going up. A banner strung between two poles reads "NIGHT KIWI: THE RUMOUR IS TRUE." Flash is on a ladder with a foam sword in his teeth. Lux is sorting bottle caps into piles on the floor. Static sits next to the boombox, testing the speakers.*

**Flash:** Oi, Lux! Hand me the banner!

**Lux:** The banner's a treasure, Flash! I'm keeping it!

**Flash:** You're not keeping the banner, Lux. It's for the party.

**Lux:** The party's a treasure, Flash!

**Flash:** Everything's a treasure to you, Lux.

**Lux:** That's because everything's shiny, bro.

*Static tests the speakers. A low hum, then a crackle, then a bass note.*

**Static:** Ok. It works. Ok.

**Lux:** It's singing, Static!

**Static:** It's humming, Lux. That's different.

**Lux:** Humming's singing for quiet people.

**Static:** Thanks, Lux. That's actually nice.

*Tiles walks onto the stage, wearing his gold jacket, carrying a box of pineapple lollies. Honey follows, clipboard in hand.*

**Tiles:** Right! Who's on the list for tonight?

**Honey:** Everybody, Tiles. It's a very long list.

**Tiles:** Honey, it's a party. Nobody reads the list at a party.

**Honey:** I read the list, Tiles. That's my job.

**Tiles:** Okay, Honey. Read it out.

**Honey:** Tiles, Glitter, Kodak, Nova, Grain, Flash, Ruby, Bando, Lux, Static, me, and Hemi Walker. Plus Priya, Bill, Mo, Ruth, Gurjit, Vito, Sal, Velvet, Mikey, Kyle, Jace, Tanner, Brooke, Pip, Hobbs, and Dennis.

**Tiles:** That's the whole city, Honey.

**Honey:** That's the whole SALT, Tiles. It's very colour-coded.

**Tiles:** What colour is Jace?

**Honey:** Yellow, Tiles. Caution.

**Tiles:** And Pip?

**Honey:** Blue. Police.

**Tiles:** And Hobbs?

**Honey:** Blue and tomato-coloured, Tiles. He's on his break.

*Ruby walks in from the side, sweaty, carrying a court net over her shoulder.*

**Ruby:** Tiles! The court's ready. Who wants to play?

**Tiles:** Ruby, it's a party. We don't play at parties.

**Ruby:** We do if I win, Tiles.

**Tiles:** Ruby, you always win.

**Ruby:** Then it's a party, bro. Everyone wins.

*Grain walks in from the playground side, quietly, holding a single toy shield. He stops in front of Tiles.*

**Grain:** Tiles. I'm coming.

**Tiles:** Grain! You're coming to a party?

**Grain:** Yeah. Not for the party. For the sandcastle.

**Tiles:** The sandcastle's been gone for weeks, Grain.

**Grain:** I know. I'm here to say I'm over it.

**Tiles:** That's big, Grain.

**Grain:** Yeah. Don't tell Nova. She'll cry.

*Nova appears behind them with a plate of biscuits, overhearing.*

**Nova:** I heard that, Grain. I'm crying.

**Grain:** Nah, Nova. Don't cry.

**Nova:** I'm crying because it's nice, Grain. You're allowed.

## Scene 4: The Market, Saturday Dusk

*The Market is lit with lanterns and fairy lights. A small crowd gathers around the stage. Bill sits in a folding chair near his pie stall, a rug over his knees. Priya stands beside him, notebook in hand. Mo tunes his guitar on a milk crate.*

**Bill:** Priya, love, how's the essay coming along?

**Priya:** It's finished, Bill. Almost. I've got the conclusion.

**Bill:** What's the conclusion?

**Priya:** That the city's not really about the buildings, or the gangs, or the tomatoes. It's about the people who keep turning up.

**Bill:** That's a very wise conclusion, love.

**Priya:** It's from Ruth, really. She said the lights are the important thing.

**Bill:** Ruth's always been the sensible one.

**Priya:** And you, Bill. You told me to write about the bench.

**Bill:** I did, love. The bench is important. It's where people sit and watch everyone else.

*Mo starts strumming. He sings a few lines of a song about a pigeon, quite badly.*

**Mo:** I'm a poet, Bill! Sixteen verses!

**Bill:** Mo, lad, you're singing about a pigeon.

**Mo:** It's a very important pigeon, Bill.

**Bill:** So's a sausage roll, son. Don't get cocky.

*Hemi walks up the Market path, blanket still around his shoulders, a stale biscuit wrapped in a napkin in one hand. He sees Bill and sits down beside him.*

**Hemi:** Evening, Bill.

**Bill:** Evening, Hemi. Still got your hat?

**Hemi:** It's in an evidence bag, Bill.

**Bill:** Shame. It suited you.

**Hemi:** It suited the rumour, Bill. Not me.

**Bill:** You're the rumour now, son. You can't escape it.

**Hemi:** I can try, Bill.

**Bill:** Try with a biscuit. It's the best disguise in the SALT.

*Hemi holds up the napkin. Bill peers in at the stale biscuit.*

**Bill:** That's a historic biscuit, son. Ruth's?

**Hemi:** It's Vito's, Bill. From the tin.

**Bill:** He'll be furious.

**Hemi:** He'll be delighted, Bill. He likes things that are a bit historic.

*Bill takes the biscuit, bites it, and looks at Hemi with great seriousness.*

**Bill:** It's terrible, lad.

**Hemi:** I know, Bill.

**Bill:** Best biscuit I've had in years.

## Scene 5: Riccarton Road, Saturday Night

*A quiet street, the dealerships dark except for the flickering white light of the Riccarton Motors sign. From a distance, music drifts up from the Market. Jace stands on a traffic cone at the kerb, looking toward the party with a faraway expression. Kyle sits on the bonnet of the ute behind him, eating a pineapple lolly.*

**Kyle:** Jace, you're going to fall off that cone.

**Jace:** I'm not falling, Kyle. I'm supervising.

**Kyle:** Supervising what?

**Jace:** The hubcaps. And the cars. And the bins. And the lollies.

**Kyle:** The lollies aren't on the Riccarton Road, Jace.

**Jace:** They're on the whole SALT, bro. That's the point.

*A pause. Music from the Market swells.*

**Kyle:** Jace, can I ask you something?

**Jace:** Go on.

**Kyle:** Why did you want the parking so badly?

**Jace:** Because it's the only thing in this town nobody ever gave us, Kyle.

**Kyle:** Mate, the parking's a car park.

**Jace:** It's not just a car park, Kyle. It's a place to park your dreams. Or at least your ute.

**Kyle:** That's the most sensible thing you've said all week.

**Jace:** Don't get used to it, bro.

*Tanner and Brooke walk up the street, arms around each other, sharing a single hokey pokey cone. They stop beside the cone and look up at Jace.*

**Tanner:** Jace! Is the party on?

**Jace:** It's on, bro. Go and get a lolly.

**Brooke:** Are we invited?

**Jace:** You're on the list, Brooke. Yellow.

**Brooke:** Yellow's good, bro. It's Pip's colour.

**Tanner:** Pip's blue, Brooke.

**Brooke:** Pip's yellow when she's having fun, Tanner.

**Jace:** Then she's having a lot of fun.

*Jace steps down off the cone and stretches. He looks at Kyle, then at the ute.*

**Jace:** Kyle. Get the ute. We're going to the party.

**Kyle:** The ute's got no hubcaps, Jace.

**Jace:** Then we'll go on the bare rims, bro. It's a statement.

**Kyle:** It's a dangerous statement, Jace.

**Jace:** Everything's dangerous, Kyle. That's why we go.

## End of Part 9b

**Threads moved forward:**

- Jace has been made a hubcap manager, on a one-month trial. Kyle is his assistant, with a lolly as a raise.
- Two hubcaps are back, one is bent, and one still has a duck footprint. Kyle is still hunting the last one.
- Glitter has invited Hemi to Tiles' party, and he has agreed to come, with a stale biscuit.
- Tiles has a full guest list and a Sunday party planned. Grain has come to say he is over the sandcastle.
- Priya has her essay conclusion: the city is about the people who keep turning up.
- Jace, Kyle, Tanner and Brooke are all heading to the party, on bare rims, in a ute with no hubcaps.
