# MosaicScope Story, Part 2: The Nor'wester

**Series:** Full conversational storyline, 10 parts. Part 1 is "The Cold Snap."

**Format:** Scenes with a location, time and cast. Speakers in bold. `[stage directions]` in brackets. Kiwi voice, cartoon tone, no slurs, no real-world politics.

**Picks up from Part 1:** The Riccarton Boys plan to crash Tiles' party at the stage. Ruth has counter-offered the Famiglia. Static has seen a green-yellow sky. Gurjit's pizza sign is in trouble. Priya's essay is still unwritten. The Night Kiwi has warned about the dairy.

---

## Scene 1: The Stage, Early Evening

*The stage behind the Market. String lights, a boombox on a milk crate, a foam sword rack, a chilly bin the size of a small car. The sky is an odd colour. Tiles stands on an upturned bucket, arms wide, giving a speech to nobody in particular.*

**Tiles:** Ladies, gentlemen, and anyone who found the wrong stage, welcome! Tonight we celebrate the fact that it hasn't rained, the Cathedral is still standing, and I haven't been arrested this week.

**Flash:** You've been arrested three times this week, cuz.

**Tiles:** Those were warnings. Warnings are a kind of arrest that doesn't cost anything.

**Glitter:** Get down off the bucket, Tiles. You look like a tomato on a stick.

**Tiles:** Tomatoes are the pride of the SALT, Glitter.

**Ruby:** Tomatoes are the pride of Vito, and he's a menace.

**Tiles:** Okay. Ruby, are you on the court or are you on my case?

**Ruby:** I'm on both. I'm multitasking, bro.

*Lux runs in from the side, carrying a plastic bag full of bottle caps. She tips them onto the stage, where they bounce and clatter.*

**Lux:** Look! I've got gold ones, silver ones, and one that's a bit bent but very special!

**Kodak:** Lux, you're going to set off Static.

**Static:** I'm fine. I'm fine. Those are just bottle caps. Please don't drop them near the decks.

**Lux:** I'm not dropping them, I'm decorating!

**Bando:** Decorating with bottle caps is a crime, Lux. Like a lot of other stuff.

**Lux:** It's art, Bando. Art is a crime if you do it right.

**Bando:** Then you're a criminal genius.

*Nova hurries over, a first aid kit under one arm and a plate of biscuits in her other hand.*

**Nova:** Okay, everyone, I brought biscuits. They're a bit stale but they're biscuits. Please don't make me cry.

**Grain:** Nah.

**Nova:** Grain, you're not allowed to say nah to biscuits.

**Grain:** It's not about the biscuits. It's about the sandcastle.

**Nova:** Grain, that was four weeks ago.

**Grain:** Four weeks, four days. Still thinking about it.

*Honey strolls in from nowhere, holding a clipboard with a colour-coded list.*

**Honey:** Oh, hiya. Sorted. Whatever you're here for, it's sorted, cuz.

**Tiles:** Honey, we don't have a list tonight. We have a party.

**Honey:** Everything has a list, Tiles. Even the parties. Especially the parties. You've got the foam swords on the left, the lollies in the middle, and the chilly bin is in the red zone.

**Tiles:** Why's the chilly bin in the red zone?

**Honey:** Because Flash is near it, bro.

**Flash:** I'm not going near the chilly bin. I'm going near the swords.

**Honey:** That's what you said last time.

*Static turns the boombox on. Music fills the air. A few heads turn toward the stage.*

**Static:** Music's on. Ok. Music's on. I'm fine.

**Glitter:** You're shaking, babe.

**Static:** It's the bass. It shakes me. In a good way.

**Glitter:** Okay. Just don't drop the boombox, Static. You know how the Nor'wester gets.

**Static:** Nor'wester's not here yet.

*Everyone looks up. The sky has turned that colour again. A cold little gust blows a paper cup across the stage.*

**Nova:** Hang on. Is that the wind?

**Kodak:** It's not hot. It's the other one. Southerly.

**Ruby:** It's both, bro. They're fighting. Like Flash and Tiles.

**Flash:** We're not fighting. We're having a foam sword disagreement.

**Tiles:** That's a fight, Flash.

**Flash:** It's a disagreement with swords.

## Scene 2: The Market, Ten Minutes Later

*Crowds drift past stalls selling pav, hokey pokey and a surprising number of cheap sunglasses. Priya is sitting on a bench with a laptop, typing furiously and looking miserable. Bill sits next to her, eating a sausage roll.*

**Priya:** Bill, I need an opening line.

**Bill:** Try, "The city of Christchurch has been under renovation since before I was born."

**Priya:** That's not an essay, Bill, that's a complaint.

**Bill:** A good essay starts with a complaint. Then it gets a bit clever.

**Priya:** My lecturer wants a critical analysis of urban identity.

**Bill:** Ah. Urban identity. That's the Park. Also the Market. Also that bench you're sitting on, love. It's been here since 1974.

**Priya:** Honestly? It's a very uncomfortable bench.

**Bill:** That's the identity, love. Uncomfortable, a bit cold, and always someone sitting next to you complaining.

**Priya:** Is that why you sit here?

**Bill:** I sit here because it's the only spot where you can see the Cathedral, the Market, and every idiot who parks on the footpath.

*A scooter zooms past, weaving between pedestrians. Lux is on it, waving a bottle cap.*

**Lux:** Hi, Priya! Hi, Bill! I'm going to find a lolly!

**Bill:** Careful, love. The footpath's not a racetrack.

**Lux:** It's a racetrack when you're fast!

*She zooms off. Priya stares after her.*

**Priya:** She's going to crash.

**Bill:** She's going to crash into something eventually. Probably a pie.

**Priya:** Bill, do you think the Riccarton Boys are going to turn up at the party?

**Bill:** Oh, they'll turn up. They always turn up to parties, same as the pigeons. Hungry and up to no good.

**Priya:** Should I go?

**Bill:** Go and write your essay, love. Write about the bench. Write about the Market. Write about the Cathedral and how it's under renovation forever. Nobody's going to mark you down for telling the truth.

**Priya:** Bill, you're really helpful.

**Bill:** Don't tell anyone. I've got a reputation to keep up.

## Scene 3: The Stage, Half an Hour Later

*The party is in full swing. People are dancing, foam swords clash, and someone has blown up an inflatable flamingo. Lights flicker. The sky is now a full-on storm colour. Static is at the boombox, looking pale.*

**Static:** Ok. Ok. I think the power's going.

**Glitter:** It's fine, Static. It's just the wind.

**Static:** The wind knocked out the zoo last year, Glitter. The penguins were not impressed.

**Glitter:** The penguins are always not impressed.

*A loud engine roars around the corner. A yellow hatchback screeches to a halt at the edge of the stage. Its bass hits so hard the foam swords on the rack start to rattle. Jace, Tanner and Brooke tumble out, grinning.*

**Jace:** Oi! Tiles! We've come to crash your party, bro!

**Tiles:** Jace, you're not on the list.

**Honey:** He's not on the list, Tiles. I have a red zone for people like him.

**Jace:** You've got a red zone, Honey? That's a bit much, bro.

**Honey:** It's colour-coded, cuz. Don't judge.

**Tanner:** Mate, the lollies. Are they in the chilly bin?

**Tiles:** Lollies are for members, Tanner.

**Tanner:** I'm a member. I've been a member since 2024.

**Tiles:** You've been a member of the Riccarton Boys since 2024, bro. Not the party.

**Brooke:** Same thing, Tiles.

**Ruby:** It's not the same thing, Brooke. It's a completely different thing. Like your tracksuit.

**Brooke:** My tracksuit's a masterpiece, Ruby.

**Ruby:** It's a crime against fashion. Say that again.

**Brooke:** Don't make me say it again, Ruby. I'll say it louder.

*The music stops with a crackle. The string lights blink out, one by one. The whole stage plunges into the orange glow of the streetlights.*

**Static:** Oh no. Oh no no no. The power's gone.

**Nova:** Everybody stay calm. Static, where's the torch?

**Static:** Kodak has the torch. Kodak has everything. Kodak's the torch.

**Kodak:** I'm not the torch, Static. I'm holding the torch. Hold still. Got it.

*A beam of torchlight swings across the stage. It lands on Jace, who is holding a chilly bin lid over his head like a shield.*

**Kodak:** Jace, what are you doing?

**Jace:** Nothing, bro. Just protecting my chicken.

**Kodak:** Mate, that's a lolly, not a chicken.

**Jace:** I'm protecting my lolly, bro. It's a lolly with feelings.

**Honey:** That's not on the list, Jace.

**Jace:** Honey, mate, put it on the list.

*Glitter stands on a crate, holding a phone up as a light. Her face is serious.*

**Glitter:** Okay, everyone listen. The power's out. The Nor'wester is coming. And the Riccarton Boys have turned up uninvited. Who wants to be the one to say that's a coincidence?

**Tanner:** It's not a coincidence, Glitter. It's destiny.

**Glitter:** Destiny's a bit rude, Tanner.

*The wind gusts hard. A string of bunting rips loose and goes flying across the stage. Something crashes in the background.*

**Tiles:** Right. Party's on pause. Everyone inside the Stage shed. Bring the lollies, bring the swords, and bring your bad attitudes.

**Flash:** Mine's already inside, bro.

**Tiles:** It's always inside, Flash. That's the problem.

## Scene 4: Inside the Stage Shed, Minutes Later

*A small corrugated iron shed behind the stage, lit by a single candle. Everyone's crammed in. Rain is pounding on the roof. Static clutches a boombox like a baby. Nova is handing out biscuits. The Riccarton Boys are sitting in a corner, looking awkward.*

**Nova:** Biscuit, Tanner?

**Tanner:** Yeah, cheers, Nova. You're a good one.

**Nova:** Thanks. Are you okay? You look a bit wobbly.

**Tanner:** Nah, I'm fine. I just don't like the dark.

**Nova:** Nobody likes the dark, Tanner. That's why we have candles.

**Tanner:** Candles are fire, Nova. Fire's worse.

**Nova:** Only if you're a moth.

*Tiles leans against the wall, arms folded, looking at Jace.*

**Tiles:** Okay, Jace. Why are you really here?

**Jace:** Parking, bro. We want the SALT parking. The Famiglia's been hogging it for years. Lollies and tomatoes, bro. It's not a parking strategy.

**Tiles:** It's not a parking strategy, Jace. It's a business.

**Jace:** It's a business for old blokes with pinstripes. We're the young ones. We need the bays.

**Tiles:** So you crash my party to make a point?

**Jace:** I crash your party because the lollies are sick.

**Glitter:** Oh my god, Jace, that's the most honest thing you've ever said.

**Jace:** Thanks, Glitter. Lovely to be appreciated.

**Tiles:** Okay. Here's my deal. You can have a lolly. You can have a biscuit. But you're not getting the SALT parking, Jace. Not tonight, not ever.

**Jace:** Oh yeah? Who says?

**Tiles:** The crew says, bro. Everyone in this shed says.

**Ruby:** Hell yeah, we say.

**Flash:** We definitely say.

**Jace:** Fine. But you owe me a favour, Tiles.

**Tiles:** I owe you nothing, Jace.

**Jace:** You owe me one burnout, bro. In the middle of Riccarton Road. Next week.

**Tiles:** That's a lot of burnout.

**Jace:** It's exactly enough.

*A bang on the roof. Everyone jumps. Lux squeals.*

**Lux:** It's a bird! It's a big bird!

**Bando:** It's a bin lid, Lux. Blown off the Market.

**Lux:** It's a flying bin lid!

**Kodak:** Smile, Lux. Got it.

## Scene 5: The Dairy, Night

*Power is still out across the SALT. The dairy is lit by a battery lantern and a camping lamp. Ruth is counting stock by candlelight, muttering. Gurjit has come over, wrapped in a tea towel, carrying a pizza box with a single slice left in it.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth, the power's out at my place too.

**Ruth:** Mine too, bro. Everyone's power's out. It's the Nor'wester's fault.

**Gurjit:** I brought you a slice of pineapple pizza.

**Ruth:** Gurjit, I love you, but I'm not eating a cold pizza with pineapple at ten at night.

**Gurjit:** It's still warm in the middle.

**Ruth:** The middle is the only warm bit, bro. That's the problem.

*Ruth sits down on a milk crate. She looks tired. She takes the slice and bites it.*

**Ruth:** You know what? That's actually good.

**Gurjit:** I know. It's my secret recipe.

**Ruth:** It's pineapple on pizza, Gurjit. It's not a secret.

**Gurjit:** Then why does everyone hate it?

**Ruth:** Because everyone's scared of change.

*The shop door rattles. Something bangs against the window. Both of them look up.*

**Ruth:** Gurjit. Did you hear that?

**Gurjit:** It's the wind. It's always the wind.

**Ruth:** The wind doesn't knock.

*Another knock. Then a voice, muffled but clear, from outside.*

**Voice (outside):** Ruth! It's Sal! Please don't freak out! I've got lollies!

**Ruth:** Oh, for crying out loud. Sal, why are you out in a Nor'wester?

**Sal:** The Don sent me, Ruth! He wants to apologise!

**Ruth:** Apologise for what?

**Sal:** For the lollies! He says they're on him! All of them!

*Ruth gets up slowly and walks to the door. She cracks it open. Sal is soaked, holding a sad, crumpled paper bag. Wind whips his hair.*

**Ruth:** You're soaking, Sal.

**Sal:** I know. The Don says the Nor'wester is a sign.

**Ruth:** A sign of what?

**Sal:** That I should be inside, but I'm not. He's a very confusing man.

**Ruth:** Come in, before you blow away.

*Sal steps inside, drips on the floor, and hands her the bag.*

**Sal:** Twelve lollies. Pineapple. Not counted. The Don says they're a gift.

**Ruth:** A gift? Sal, that's the same bag from Tuesday.

**Sal:** It's the same bag. He just gave it back. Says the protection rate's gone up, but he's willing to be reasonable.

**Ruth:** Reasonable? Sal, the man once tried to buy my car for a lolly.

**Sal:** That was a joke, Ruth. Mostly.

**Gurjit:** Ruth, what's going on?

**Ruth:** Sal's brought a bag of lollies and a bad attitude, bro.

**Sal:** I brought lollies, not an attitude. My attitude's at the bakery.

*Ruth opens the bag and looks in. There's a folded note at the bottom. She pulls it out and reads it by lantern light.*

**Ruth:** Sal. What does this say?

**Sal:** It says "The Don is watching."

**Ruth:** That's creepy, Sal.

**Sal:** That's what I said. He says it's a bit creepy. That's the point.

*A dark shape passes the window, fast, up on the roof. Something lands with a soft thump. Everyone freezes.*

**Gurjit:** Oh god. Oh god. Is that the Night Kiwi?

**Ruth:** Shh. Listen.

*A dry voice comes through the wind, from somewhere above.*

**Night Kiwi (off-screen):** Watch the dairy, Ruth. Tonight's the night.

**Ruth:** Watch it for what?

**Night Kiwi (off-screen):** Someone's going to try to take the parking. It's going to start here.

*Silence. Then the sound of footsteps retreating across the roof.*

**Gurjit:** Ruth, I'm going to be honest. I'm really scared.

**Ruth:** So am I, bro. But at least the pizza's warm.

## Scene 6: Riccarton Road, Late Night

*Power is out in patches. The traffic lights are dead, blinking yellow. Pip's patrol car pulls over. Hobbs is in the passenger seat, eating a pie.*

**Hobbs:** Pip, why are we sitting in the rain?

**Pip:** Because someone's going to do a burnout, Sarge. I can feel it in my bones.

**Hobbs:** You can feel the burnout in your bones?

**Pip:** It's a police thing, Sarge. Like a sixth sense, but with more torque.

**Hobbs:** Right. And what's the plan if the burnout turns up?

**Pip:** We pull them over, give them a warning, and then we give them a ticket.

**Hobbs:** Then what?

**Pip:** Then we get the tomato back from Vito.

**Hobbs:** The tomato's not Vito's, Pip. It's mine.

**Pip:** It's on your dashboard, Sarge.

**Hobbs:** It's on loan. Vito's very particular about his tomatoes.

*A roar comes down Riccarton Road. The yellow hatchback comes screaming round the corner, lights blinking, bass shaking the windows. Pip flicks on her siren.*

**Pip:** Got them!

**Hobbs:** Pip, put your seatbelt on.

**Pip:** I'm already in pursuit, Sarge!

*The hatchback slides sideways at the lights, does a perfect burnout, and roars away. Tanner is waving out of the window, whooping.*

**Tanner:** Yeah, Pip! Smell that? That's respect!

**Pip:** That's smoke, Tanner! Pull over!

**Brooke (off-screen):** He won't pull over, officer! He's a legend!

**Hobbs:** Pip. Stop the car.

**Pip:** I can't, Sarge. I'm in the zone.

**Hobbs:** You're not in the zone, Pip. You're in a puddle.

*The patrol car skids to a halt. Pip looks down. The car is sitting in a deep puddle. She sighs.*

**Pip:** Sarge, I think we've been beaten by a yellow hatchback.

**Hobbs:** We've been beaten by a yellow hatchback and a bad sense of direction. Put it in the report.

**Pip:** Under what heading?

**Hobbs:** "Riccarton Boys, still at large, and the Nor'wester's a menace."

## End of Part 2

**Story threads moved forward:**

- The party at the stage ended in a blackout and a shed. Jace has a "burnout favour" owed to Tiles, and Tiles has refused him the SALT parking.
- Ruth accepted a lolly bag from Sal, with a note from Vito that reads "The Don is watching." Her protection rate is now up for negotiation.
- The Night Kiwi has warned that the parking fight will "start at the dairy." Something is coming to the SALT.
- Pip and Hobbs lost the Riccarton hatchback in a puddle and are short one tomato.
- Priya got her essay opening from Bill: a complaint about the Cathedral, and a bench.
- Static is still worried, but the power is back on in some places.

**Next:** Part 3 covers the morning after the storm, the dairy negotiation with the Famiglia and the Riccarton Boys, and the first sighting of the Velvet gala invitation.
